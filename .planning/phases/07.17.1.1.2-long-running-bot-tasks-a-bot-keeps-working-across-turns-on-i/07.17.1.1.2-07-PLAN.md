## Phase Goal

See `07.17.1.1.2-01-PLAN.md` (PD-05, PD-07, PD-09, PD-13, PD-15). **This plan's slice:** which turn gets which task
tools and what it knows about the task: `taskOfferFor`, the `task` trigger in `buildMentionEvents`, the tools,
`turn.task` and the ledger link in `turnPolicy`.

<objective>
Add the `task` trigger and `AgentMention.task` to the chat-agent bridge, `taskOfferFor` (and its helpers) to a new
`agent-channel/task-offer.ts`, the trigger's rules (browser off, attachments listed, Work Zone offer, the daily-limit
callback) to `buildMentionEvents`, and the task tools, `turn.task` and `agent_turns.task_id` to `turnPolicy`, gated by
`computeTurn.longTasks` (D-02, D-04, D-07, D-09, D-10).

Purpose: 08's routes accept a call only on a turn that carries the tool; 09, 11 and 12 deliver continuations through this
exact path.
Output: one new module, three edited ones, one integration file.
</objective>
