## Shared Patterns

- **Egress:** every outbound call `egress.bound(policy)` / `oauthPolicy(sameOrigin)`; enforced by `ocw.raw-egress` (already covers `connectors/` and `agent-channel/connectors.ts`).
- **Tenant tx:** `core.db.withTenant({ organizationId, userId })` only; org principal uses `userId: null` or the acting admin.
- **Sealed secrets:** `app.secrets` kind `connector_credential`, AAD names org/principal/server; DTOs expose `hasSecret` only.
- **Errors:** closed codes only (`ConnectorErrorCode`, new `ConnectorAuthFailure`); never provider `error_description` (callback ignores it, routes.ts l.92-98).
- **Audit:** new actions `connector.org_connected|org_disconnected|org_needs_reconnect`; proxied calls keep `bot.tool_call(.completed)` (`agent-channel/claim.ts` l.115, 180, 218).
- **Permissions:** `perms.can(req, tx, "mcp.manage")`, re-decided at the callback.
