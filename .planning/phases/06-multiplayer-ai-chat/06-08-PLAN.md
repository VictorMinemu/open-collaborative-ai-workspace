## Phase Goal

**As a** member who holds a private AI chat, **I want to** share it in place with a colleague, a team, a department or the whole organization so that several of us and the model write in the same conversation — the model knowing who said what and answering one request at a time on the asker's own plan or model, nobody's private context leaking, and agents joining as full personas that post live cards — **so that** AI work in the workspace becomes multiplayer, attributable and safe.

**This plan's slice:** shared chats live where people look — the sidebar, unread badges, the inbox and notifications — with each participant's own view. P-decisions: `06-01-PLAN.md` (P-09).

<objective>
Make shared AI chats first-class in the sidebar and inbox: live per-recipient summaries, unread for joined chats with a read marker that also joins unit/org grantees, a share notification for direct person grants, mention notifications inside shared chats.

Purpose: MULTI-01 (discoverability of what was shared), MULTI-02 (participants in realtime) — D-03; P-09.
Output: `notifyUsers`, `emitChatUpdated`, `POST /ai-chats/:id/read`, unread on AI chat summaries, inbox support, web sidebar/inbox changes, `shared.int.test.ts`, `ai-chat-shared-inbox.spec.ts`.
</objective>

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-06-08-01 | Information disclosure | notifications or sidebar summaries revealing a chat after revoke | high | mitigate | Inbox filtered by `readableIds` incl. ai_chat at read time; access-null event removes the item; tests after revoke (Task 1) |
| T-06-08-02 | Information disclosure | per-recipient summary leaking owner-only settings | medium | mitigate | Summaries carry no settings; per-access summaries; test (Task 2) |
