# Phase 07.17.1.1.2 Plan 09: The continuation Summary

**A bot that calls `ocw_task_continue` now gets its next turn by itself. When the cell reports the turn done, the platform delivers exactly one `agent.task.v1` in the task card's thread, for the person who asked, who also pays for it. Every budget, the policy, that person's access, the bot and the card are checked again under the task's row lock before each turn, so the same wait never produces two turns. Taint stays on for the rest of the task, an idle turn gets one nudge before the task stops, and a minute tick recovers lost jobs and lost turns.**

## Intended change (CLAUDE.md plan mode: permissions, audit, tenant data)

- **New module `agent-channel/task-wakes.ts`.** A structural copy of 07.16's Work Zone wake, in three transactions:
  - **decide**: no user, `FOR UPDATE` on the task, RLS plus an explicit organization filter.
  - **deliver**: as the requester, so their rights are decided again (ADR-0019, D-07).
  - **release**: no user. It restores the reserved fields exactly.
- **No new permission, grain or RLS change.** No check was loosened.
  - The delivery runs `buildMentionEvents` → `turnPolicy`, the same path as a person's turn: `computeTurn`, the daily meter, taint and the tool caps.
  - A task never widens a turn (P3).
- **Audit.** `bot.task_continued` carries only `{ taskId, conversationId, eventId, turnsUsed, maxTurns, reason }`.
  - Stops reuse 08's `bot.task_stopped` (ids, outcome, counts).
  - Logs carry ids, counts and error names. Jobs carry `{ organizationId, taskId }` only (P1, P7).
- **The cell's done.** `POST /turns/:eventId/done` now also releases a task's wait. It only touches this organization's own turn of the bot named in the body: tx0 binds the event, then `taskTurnDone` filters again by organization and agent.

## What was built

- **`taskEventId(taskId, waitSeq)`**: a UUIDv8 from SHA-256 of `ocw.bot_task.turn\0<taskId>\0<waitSeq>`, the same recipe as `wakeEventId`.
- **`continueBotTask(core, deps, { organizationId, taskId })`**:
  - **decide.** Only a `waiting` task whose `wake_at` has come, and whose current turn reported done (or is older than 30 min), goes on. The id comes from `wait_seq`. `delivered()` confirms a delivery whose outcome was lost.
  - **decide, stops.** Under the lock: every turn used (`budget_turns`), past the deadline (`budget_time`), long tasks off (`policy_off`), the bot archived (`bot_unavailable`, no message), the requester deactivated (`access_lost`, no message).
  - **decide, taint.** A tainted earlier turn of the task taints the next turn and the task row.
  - **decide, reservation.** `consumeWait`, then the card shows Working.
  - **deliver.** The requester still reads the conversation (a thread through its parent), the bot is live and a member, the card is not deleted.
    - The mention is built on the card message (`threadRootId`: the card in a DM or group, the task's thread elsewhere).
    - It uses `trigger: "task"`, the fixed `taskContinueSentence` and `onLimitReached`.
    - Then the session-output mark, `agent.task.v1` on `cell:<org>` and `bot.task_continued`.
  - **release.** An exception gives the turn back and throws, so pg-boss retries. A refusal gives the turn back and stops the task: `daily_limit` with its message; `access_lost` and `bot_unavailable` with a card notice only.
- **`taskTurnDone`**:
  - `done_at` is set on the first report.
  - A `turn` or `nudge` wait is shortened to 5 s, and one job is queued at `greatest(wake_at, now + 5 s)`.
  - A task still `running` on this very turn gets D-12: one nudge turn, then `no_progress`.
- **`tickBotTasks`**: reads `app.due_bot_tasks`, then handles each task in its own organization's transaction: a disabled or archived bot, a past deadline, a lost job (it queues one), a lost turn (D-12).
- **Registration.** `registerTaskWakes` (the continuation worker, plus the `bot_task.tick` worker on `* * * * *`) sits next to `registerWorkSessionWakes`. `turnDone` calls `taskTurnDone` before the chain branch.

## Deviations from Plan

### Auto-fixed Issues

**1. [Rule 1 - Bug] A refused delivery would have counted a turn that never ran**
- **Found during:** Task 2 (daily limit).
- **Issue:** `consumeWait` in decide already adds 1 to `turns_used`. Stopping straight after a refusal would have recorded a turn that never ran.
- **Fix:** Every refusal releases first (the fields are restored exactly), then stops. The test asserts that `turns_used` stays at 1 after a `daily_limit` stop.
- **Commit:** ca8b92a6, 1b34b780

**2. [Rule 3 - Blocking] `drainTaskJobs` needed to pass the 5 s gap**
- **Found during:** Task 1. The done sets `wake_at` to now + 5 s, so a forced job ran before the wait was due.
- **Fix:** The helper also moves waits that are due within 6 s to `now()`. Timers and backstops are left alone, so their readiness is really tested; `setTask` moves those.
- **Also changed:** the helper drains only `bot_task.continue`. The tick is never queued in tests, because workers are off.
- **Commit:** 3d2c0ad4

**3. Smaller choices (no planned behaviour dropped)**
- **Deactivated requester.** Checked in decide, without a user (the wake does the same). `isReadable` stays in deliver.
- **The session-output mark** is scoped to the task's own turns. Plan 12's Work Zone wake keeps its own rule.
- **Taint in tests.** It is set through the real `claimToolCall` with an untrusted-output flag (the `/tool-calls` path), not with an attachment read. The Work Zone read is recorded the same way, as an allowed `ocw_work_session_read` claim.
- **The plan's people.** Sam is the plan's Ana and Alex is Ben, the sandbox fixture's people. The deactivation case uses a newly invited member (Dana), so the shared clients keep their sessions.
