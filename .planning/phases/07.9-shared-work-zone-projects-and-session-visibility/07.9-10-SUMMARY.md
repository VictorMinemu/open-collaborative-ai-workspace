# Phase 07.9 Plan 10: Who can see a session, from its header Summary

**One chip, one panel.** The owner narrows a session from its header in one click. The person removed sees why on
their screen within 2 s and the row leaves their list. Undo gives it back in one request. Everyone who can see the
session can ask who else can, and gets faces, a count and the always-on line (admins, device owner). This works at
1440 and 390, light and dark.

## Deviations from Plan

1. **[Process] Tasks 1 and 2 in one commit.** The panel was written once, in its final shape (pickers included), so
   the tracer commit carries both. TDD:
   - The panel's static-markup suite and the `setAudience` tests were written first and failed for the expected
     reason.
   - The e2e tracer was written before the components. Its RED is by construction (no `visibility-chip` existed) and
     was not run separately, to save one ~6 min isolated run.
   - `pnpm verify` must pass before every commit, so RED was never committed on its own (same as 06 and 08).
2. **[Interface] `VisibilityChip({ session, viewerId, canChange })`** instead of `{ session, me }`. `canChange` is the
   detail's `work_session.visibility` and only words the accessible name ("Change" or "See"); the panel itself follows
   `viewerCan.change`. Draft mode's `value` is a `DraftAudience` that carries names (see decisions).
3. **[Rule 2] Touch scope.** `expectTouchTargets` runs on the sheet (all controls ≥ 44 px, including a 44 px close
   that replaces the primitive's 32 px one) and a dedicated hit-area probe runs on the chip. It does not run on the
   whole header: the existing Queue, Changes and ⋯ buttons are 32 px, and the plan forbids restyling the header. They
   are broken-window candidate 1.
4. **[Rule 2] "Open session" after Undo.** The access-lost view watches the session list. When the session comes back
   and the cause is `visibility`, it offers Open session, which remounts the live view (UI-SPEC Interaction "Narrowing
   and live removal"). The existing Back to Work Zone button is kept.
5. **[Rule 1] The self-removal warning must not check a role in the web.** `guard.test.ts` refuses `isAdmin` and role
   literals in `apps/web`. The rule (`loseAccessOnChange`, unit-tested) therefore reads only the API's viewer list:
   - It asks someone listed as lead, member or team who chooses Only {owner}, or people without themselves.
   - It never asks the owner, the device owner, or anyone the API does not list (an admin-only viewer).
   - An admin who is also a project member gets one unnecessary confirmation. The Teams case is not covered; see
     broken-window candidate 3.
6. **[Copy] Personal people search.** The UI-SPEC gives "Add people in {project}". A personal repository says "Add
   people who can use {device}", matching its People line, and its no-match line says the same.
7. **[Ownership] Live panel refresh.** The plan's "another editor's change updates the open panel" needed a
   `ws:` listener. `use-live-session.ts` belongs to 07, so the open panel adds its own listener on the multiplexed
   `ws:<id>` subscription. It refetches the view on `work_session.visibility_changed` or `work_session.updated`, and
   runs only while the panel is open. A PUT refused with 403 also refetches, so lost edit rights switch the panel to
   read-only in place.

No existing assertion was changed. No existing UI or behaviour was removed: the old access-lost copy stays for every
cause but `visibility`, and the agent bar and footer stay around the D-12 line.

## Broken-window candidates (not written to WINDOWS.md, per the coordinator)

1. **Session header controls below 44 px at 390.** Queue, Changes and ⋯ (`size="sm"` / `icon-sm`) are 32 px. This is
   pre-existing and out of scope (the header may not be restyled).
2. **The viewer reason prefers "Member" over the team.** A project member who is also in a chosen team is listed as
   "Member" in See all for a Teams session, although membership alone would not show it. This is 06's
   `readers`/`sortReaders` ordering. The spec avoids the case: Pia reaches the project only through Web.
3. **Self-removal through Teams is not warned.** The view does not say whether the editor is in a chosen team. An
   additive `viewerCan.keepsAccessAfter`-style hint, or a preview for existing sessions, would close it.
4. **Project line count after narrowing.** The view counts only the current choice, so a session opened while not on
   Project shows "Everyone in {project}" without a number. An additive `projectCount` in
   `WorkSessionVisibilityView` would fix it; it needs a contract change.
5. **Sheet primitive close is 32 px.** `ui/sheet.tsx`'s built-in close is under 44 px for every bottom sheet that
   keeps it (the AI-chat participants sheet among them). This plan works around it locally.
6. **The e2e runs overwrite other plans' screenshots.** The shared screenshot directory is written by every spec in a
   run (the 09 marker spec matches the `work-zone-visibility` filter). Executors must restore them by hand. A
   per-spec prefix guard in `shot()` would prevent it.
