## Phase Goal

See `07.16-01-PLAN.md` (PD-13, PD-15, E2, E3). **This plan's slice:** the bot keeps orchestrating its own session (follow-up
prompts, stop) through the same queue and archive people use, while people in the audience keep full control.

<objective>
Add `POST /work-sessions/prompt` and `POST /work-sessions/stop` to the bot routes and `BotSessions.prompt`/`stop`, both
through the controller functions of 10, with the own-session rule, the prompt cap, the refusals, audits, co-control
proofs, tenant and leak sweeps (D-05, D-06, D-07, D-14, PD-13).

Purpose: BWZ-02 "sends follow-up prompts … and stops them; humans of the session's audience keep prompting and stopping
the same session (one executor, many controllers)".
Output: two routes, two service functions, one integration file.
</objective>
