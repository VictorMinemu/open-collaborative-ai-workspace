# Phase 07.17 Plan 13: @team in the Web Summary

Members can now find teams and departments in the channel composer, are asked before notifying more than 20 people, and see team chips, inbox lines and unit handles.

## What was built

- **Directory.** `useOrgDirectory` reads `GET /units` and adds teams and departments whose handle no person or bot holds (`mentionUnitsOf`). Their handles join the chip map. `useMentionUnits()` exposes the list.
- **Composer.** The new `unitMentions` prop adds a "Teams" group after people, bots and broadcasts. Each row shows a round muted users/building icon, the name, a muted `@handle` and a right-aligned "Team · 6" / "Department · 24". Rows are 44 px on phones, and keyboard navigation is shared with the other rows. Only `channel-view` and its `ThreadPanel` (board card threads included) pass `unitMentions`, so DMs, group DMs and AI chats are unchanged.
- **Confirmation.** `mentionConfirmation` reuses the existing dialog. When a named team reaches more than `TEAM_MENTION_LIMITS.confirmAbove`, the dialog reads "Notify 24 people?" / "@ops reaches 24 people who can read this channel." (`team-mention-confirm`, Cancel / Send). When `@channel` also applies, its dialog wins and is unchanged.
- **Chip.** A unit target renders the mention chip with a small group icon, titled "Logistics · team". It turns amber when the viewer is in reach.
- **Inbox.** A `team_mention` item reads "mentioned @logistics", or "mentioned your team" when the unit is gone.
- **Organization page.** Tree rows show `@handle` in muted mono after team and department names (`unit-handle`, title "Mention handle"). The handle gives way to the name on narrow rows.

## Deviations from Plan

### Auto-fixed Issues

**1. [Rule 1 - Bug] Unit tree rows widened the page at 390 px**
- **Found during:** visual check after Task 2
- **Issue:** The tree is a CSS grid with an auto track, so a long unit name (and now its handle) set the track's minimum width. The page scrolled sideways (449 px before this plan with a long name, 666 px with handles).
- **Fix:** `min-w-0` on tree rows. The handle keeps 48 px (56 px in the composer) and shrinks before the name, so it never collapses to "@.".
- **Files modified:** apps/web/components/organization/unit-tree.tsx, apps/web/components/chat/composer.tsx
- **Commit:** 3491f68c

**2. [Plan detail] The handle is in unit-tree.tsx / unit-meta.tsx, not org-structure.tsx**
- The unit rows live in `unit-tree.tsx`, so the acceptance grep `unit-handle` matches `unit-meta.tsx` (the `UnitHandle` component), not `org-structure.tsx`. The detail header was left alone, so `unit-handle` stays unique on screen for 07.17-18's locator.

**3. [Design] 24 px unit icon instead of 32 px**
- The unit icon is 24 px to match the person avatars and broadcast icons in the same list (PD-19 said 32 px).

**4. [Rule 2] Channel members win over units in channel-view**
- `channel-view` filters `unitMentions` by channel member handles and lets members override unit chips. This keeps the API's precedence for bots that are in a channel but not in the org directory (personal or disabled).
