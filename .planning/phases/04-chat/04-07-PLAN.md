## Phase Goal

**As a** member of a team that works in the workspace, **I want to** hold my whole daily conversation there — channels grouped by department and team, DMs and group DMs with people and bots, messages I can edit, delete, react to, pin, save and link to, @channel/@here and notifications I control, search and files — **so that** I do not need a second chat tool and every message stays attributed, editable under audit and delivered in realtime.

**This plan's slice (tracer):** Ada opens a DM with Sam from the New message picker; it appears in both sidebars and messages flow live (contracts → `POST /dms` → `dm_key` → sidebar → DM view → browser). Then group and mixed DMs with bots, and the privacy suite. Planner decisions P-01 … P-12 are in `04-01-PLAN.md` (P-03, P-05, P-06, P-08, P-09).

<objective>
Extend the 3.1 DM model to human DMs and group DMs (humans, team bots, mixed) with fixed membership, bot-on-mention behaviour, memory off for multi-human conversations, and a privacy suite proving non-participant admins see nothing.

Purpose: CHAT-04 with D-01 and D-06.
Output: `POST /api/v1/dms`, counterpart union in `GET /api/v1/dms`, group kind across chat/agent paths, NewDmDialog, Direct messages sidebar section, DM/group headers, privacy and group suites, `e2e/tests/dms.spec.ts`.
</objective>
