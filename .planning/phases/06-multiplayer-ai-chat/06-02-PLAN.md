## Phase Goal

**As a** member who holds a private AI chat, **I want to** share it in place with a colleague, a team, a department or the whole organization so that several of us and the model write in the same conversation — the model knowing who said what and answering one request at a time on the asker's own plan or model, nobody's private context leaking, and agents joining as full personas that post live cards — **so that** AI work in the workspace becomes multiplayer, attributable and safe.

**This plan's slice (tracer, API level — the phase tracer):** Víctor shares his AI chat with Alex (person grant, can chat) → the resolver lets Alex read it and subscribe to `conv:<id>` → Alex writes → the model answers Alex on Alex's own ChatGPT plan with a roster, attributed blocks and a turn context naming Alex → both subscribers see the stream and the final message. Proven by one integration test on real PostgreSQL + Redis and the in-process fake. The browser joins in 06-03. Planner decisions P-01 … P-16 are in `06-01-PLAN.md`.

<objective>
Open AI chats to grants through the single resolver seam, make sends by grantees work on their own backend with a per-message context record, give the model structural participant identity in shared chats (solo unchanged), and keep every owner-only capability owner-only.

Purpose: MULTI-01 (person grants), MULTI-02, MULTI-03 (tracer subset), MULTI-06 (D-01, D-03, D-04, D-06; P-03 … P-07).
Output: `grants.ts`; `aiAccessSql`/`channelFacts`/readable set for AI chats; permission split; multiplayer prompt mode; `multiplayer.int.test.ts`.
</objective>
