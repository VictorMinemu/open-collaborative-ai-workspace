# Phase 07.15: Bots read attachments Verification Report

**Phase Goal:** People can attach files where they talk to bots (personal bot DMs, team bot DMs, channels/threads with
bots, AI chats with personas) and the bot actually reads them: images for image-capable models, text-like documents
(PDF included) as extracted text, any file into the bot's sandbox workspace. Nothing becomes readable to anyone who
could not already read the conversation.
