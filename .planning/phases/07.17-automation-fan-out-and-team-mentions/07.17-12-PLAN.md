## Phase Goal

See `07.17-01-PLAN.md` (PD-14). **This plan's slice:** `@team` reaches people: the members of the unit's subtree who can read
the conversation get a mention notification (success criterion 3).

<objective>
Expand unit mentions to their subtree's active members (≤ 200 per message), offer them `team_mention` in
`computeRecipients` below direct mentions, keep only readers through `eligible`, store `unit_id`, return `unit` in the
inbox, and prove audience, ranks, cap, edits, bots and tenant isolation (D-07, D-13, D-18, PD-14).

Purpose: AFO-04's "its members who can read the conversation get a mention notification".
Output: one new module, recipients and service changes, a unit test and one integration file.
</objective>
