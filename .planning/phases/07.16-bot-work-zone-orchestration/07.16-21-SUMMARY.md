# Phase 07.16 Plan 21: The bot Work Zone journey in the browser Summary

**The bot Work Zone journey now has a Playwright spec for desktop 1440 and mobile 390. A team bot gets a profile and a device consent, starts its own session from a channel mention, shows a live card that links to the session and back, is woken when the turn ends and mentions the asker. Approvals go to people, outside content becomes a proposal, and bots without a profile are unchanged. The spec runs on the e2e fake cell, which now speaks the four session tools and the wake event. A real web bug surfaced and was fixed: the proposal card never showed the outside-content warning.**

## Accomplishments

- Fake cell (PD-26): `ocw_work_session_start|prompt|read|stop` post the plugin's bodies (start sends a choice only when the directive gives it, and never a device or user key). They render `started <project> (<mode>)`, `proposed`, `queued`, `read <status>: <last output line>`, `stopped` and `refused (<code>)`. On `agent.work_session.v1`, `turn_done` reads the session and replies `Done: <last line> @<asker>` on the card. `waiting_approval` replies `Needs approval: <tool> @<deciders>`. Other transitions reply `<transition>: <summary> @<asker>`. Other specs' events and replies are unchanged.
- Spec `work-zone-bot-orchestration.spec.ts`, serial, both projects:
  1. Desktop only: the admin saves Atlas's profile in the bot sheet and allows Atlas with Full access in Settings › Devices.
  2. Phone only: the profile and consent are seeded through the API. The Work Zone section and the bot-consent block are checked at 390 (touch targets, no sideways scroll, axe, captures).
  3. Ana asks in the channel. The live card appears with no Start press, held on "Working" by the hold file. Open session shows "Back to #channel" with the exact permalink, which goes back to the highlighted card. After release the card says "Turn 1 done". The woken bot reads and replies "Done: Mode: fullAccess @ana-0716", and Ana's Mentions count reaches 1.
  4. A Supervised session shows "Approval" on the card. The bot replies "Needs approval: Write @admin" and the session still waits.
  5. After an `ocw_attachment_read`, the start is proposed and the card shows the notice. Ana starts it, and the first prompt shows "After outside content".
  6. Sam's personal bot has no profile. It proposes with `ocw_start_work_zone`, its turn has no session tool and no `workZone`, and review, Start and the session behave as before.
- Helpers: `bot-work-zone.ts` handles policy, profile and consent through the public API, and the policy is restored in `afterAll`. `work-zone-device.ts` gains `extraProjects` and `startIsolatedServer`.

## Issues Encountered

- **The local e2e was not run.** `scripts/e2e-isolated.sh` adopts the compose project of the PostgreSQL that publishes `OCW_POSTGRES_PORT`. On this machine the only such containers are the owner's demo, `t3code-81d72154-postgres-1` (127.0.0.1:54329) and `t3code-81d72154-redis-1`. The script would run `docker compose exec` in that project (drop and recreate `ocw_e2e`, `FLUSHDB` on Redis DB 9), which breaks the "never touch t3code-81d72154" rule. Free disk was 8.5 GB, not the expected 17 GB. The spec is left to the Woodpecker e2e workflow (desktop and mobile) on the PR, so no pass is claimed.
- **What CI should watch:** the selectors were checked statically against the components, but the spec has never run.
  - The device and model `Select` options are picked by visible text.
  - The live card's refresh timing is about 3 s per transition (60 s timeouts).
  - The wake reply relies on the fake runtime's last output line, `Mode: fullAccess`.
