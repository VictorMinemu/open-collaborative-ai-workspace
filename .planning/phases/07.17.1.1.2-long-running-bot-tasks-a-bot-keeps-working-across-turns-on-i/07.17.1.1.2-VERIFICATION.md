# Phase 07.17.1.1.2: Long-running bot tasks Verification Report

**Phase Goal:** A bot takes on a request that needs many turns and keeps working on it by itself until it is done,
without a person re-prompting: it starts a task, the platform delivers its next turn in the same thread, it is woken
when its long sandbox processes or its Work Zone sessions finish (or after a timer), it reports progress on a live task
card people can stop, and budgets (turns, hours, running tasks, the daily limit) stop it so it never runs away. No more
"no tengo ejecución en segundo plano".
