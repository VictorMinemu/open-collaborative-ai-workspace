## Phase Goal

**As an** Organization Admin and every member of the workspace, **I want to** arrange the company into departments, teams and projects with the four PRD roles, have every route decided by one permission resolver, and manage my own password, sessions and profile, **so that** each person and agent can do exactly what their role, units and rules allow and every sensitive change is attributed and audited.

**This plan's slice:** chat (channels, messages, threads, membership, bot replies and the conv topic) is decided by the resolver, research inventory M1–M10 and R8.

<objective>
Migrate the chat module to the permission service from 02-02 (ORG-03, research inventory M1–M10, R8), expose per-channel `viewerCan`, apply the agent ∩ human rule to bot replies, and record the real client kind on messages (AUTH-05). Behaviour seen by users does not change: same status codes and error codes (`not_a_member`, 404 for hidden channels, 410 for archived).

Purpose: SC3 requires channels and messages to be decided by the single resolver with no ad-hoc check left.
Output: chat routes on perms, deleted helpers canRead / requireReadable / requireWritable, the optional bridge field 02-10 will pass, updated web archive gating.
</objective>
