## Phase Goal

A shared agent acts with the credentials of the person who asked: each member connects their own MCP, ERP or CRM
account once, sessions refresh themselves, secrets are used by reference and never seen by the model, and people start
their own agent from a template (ROADMAP Phase 07.4).

**This plan's slice (tracer, contract level):** a per-member connector call is expressible end to end in contracts
(auth mode → turn tool + withheld reason → Agent Channel body → refusal codes), then the member, keychain and template
DTOs, then the permission grains.
