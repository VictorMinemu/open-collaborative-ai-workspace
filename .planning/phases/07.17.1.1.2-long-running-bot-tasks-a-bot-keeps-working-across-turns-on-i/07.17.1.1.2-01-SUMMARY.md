## Deviations from Plan

### Auto-fixed Issues

**1. [Rule 3 - Blocking] `@ocw/api` typecheck broke on the new card kind**
- **Found during:** Task 2 (`pnpm typecheck --force`).
- **Issue:** `insertCard` passes `AgentCardInput | PlatformCardInput` `kind` into `messageCards.kind`, whose Drizzle enum (owned by 07.17.1.1.2-03) does not list `task` yet. The error was TS2769 in `card-actions.ts`.
- **Fix:** I added `type StoredCardKind = (typeof messageCards.$inferInsert)["kind"]` and cast the insert's `kind` to it. The cast is documented and becomes an identity once 03 widens the enum. Until then the `message_cards_kind_check` CHECK still refuses a `task` row. I also widened the existing guard: a `task` card that is not platform-owned is refused like a `browser` card (same error text for `browser`). I did not touch the schema file, because 03 owns it in this wave.
- **Files modified:** `apps/api/src/modules/chat/card-actions.ts`
- **Verification:** `pnpm typecheck --force` reports 11/11 successful, and the unit and web suites are green.
- **Committed in:** `0e287139`
- **Follow-up:** 07.17.1.1.2-03 (or 08) may drop the `StoredCardKind` cast once `task` is in the column enum.

**2. [Rule 2 - Completeness] Mirror `BOT_TASK_LIMITS` includes `finishedProcessesMax`**
- The plan listed four members. The mirror's `TurnTask` needs the bound of 10, so the mirror carries it too, with the same value. The parity test pins the key set.
