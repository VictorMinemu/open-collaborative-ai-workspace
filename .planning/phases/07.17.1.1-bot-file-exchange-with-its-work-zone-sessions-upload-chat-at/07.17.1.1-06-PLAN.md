## Phase Goal

See `07.17.1.1-01-PLAN.md` (PD-11, PD-21). **This plan's slice:** the two configurable limits are stored, validated
against the server's ceiling and the organization's value, shown with what the settings need, and computed in one place.

<objective>
Add `botFileMaxBytes` to the organization's Work Zone policy (read, save, audit, ceiling), `fileMaxBytes` to the bot's
Work Zone profile (validate ≤ the organization's, store, view with `limits`), and `loadBotFileLimit`, the one function
every later call uses (D-07).

Purpose: 08 and 11 decide every call with `loadBotFileLimit`; 10 tells the bot its limit; 09 edits both fields.
Output: three source files and two extended integration files.
</objective>
