## Phase Goal

**As a** member collaborating in a channel or a Work Zone session, **I want to** see who else is here right now (active or away), get my colleagues' messages, typing and presence within half a second, and stop receiving a conversation the moment I lose access to it, **so that** shared conversations feel live and never keep streaming to someone who was removed.

**This plan's slice (RT-04, Away):** the "Away" state, end to end, from the browser's visibility and idle time to every other person's header and member list.

<objective>
Add the Away state per D-04: a client-side activity monitor, the `presence` client op with throttling, the server-side state change with a rate cap, the provider wiring in the web, and a two-browser e2e with screenshots.

Purpose: SC2 "Presence (online/away) … appears per conversation" — 03-01 delivered "online" and expiry; this plan delivers "away".
Output: contracts client op, `realtime-client/src/activity.ts`, `client.setPresence`, gateway `presence` op and `presence.setState`, reporter in `RealtimeProvider`, `presence-away.spec.ts`.
</objective>

<threat_model>
## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| browser → /ws `presence` op | Untrusted state changes at any rate |
| other people's browsers ← away/online deltas | Coarse activity of colleagues becomes visible |

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-03-02-01 | Spoofing | `presence` op | high | mitigate | The op carries only `state`; the gateway applies it to the sending connection's own entries (user from the JWT, conn from the server); Zod strips unknown fields |
| T-03-02-02 | Denial of service | presence fan-out | medium | mitigate | Change-only application, 30 changes per 10 s per socket with `rate_limited`, client leading + trailing throttle of 500 ms; integration test |
| T-03-02-03 | Tampering | malformed state | low | mitigate | ClientFrame Zod enum (online, away); `bad_frame` on anything else; test |
| T-03-02-04 | Information disclosure | activity patterns of colleagues | low | accept | Only online/away inside conversations the viewer may read, no timestamps or idle durations are published; product requirement (RT-04) |
</threat_model>
