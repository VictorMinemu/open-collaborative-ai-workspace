## Phase Goal

An automation watches a source (new orders through an MCP every 10 minutes) and, for each new item, opens its own
conversation (a thread in a channel or a card on a board), where the bot reads the context, works in the Work Zone through
its 07.16 profile, moves the card through the columns and mentions a team or a person when it finishes or needs help
(ROADMAP Phase 07.17; AFO-01…AFO-05; CONTEXT D-01…D-18).

**This plan's slice (tracer, contract tier):** the phase's vocabulary in `packages/contracts` and its mirror in the
plugin. Plus this hub: plan map, shared files, cross-plan names, planner decisions PD-01…PD-26, edge coverage,
prohibitions, flagged assumptions, source coverage. Every later 07.17 plan cites this hub.
