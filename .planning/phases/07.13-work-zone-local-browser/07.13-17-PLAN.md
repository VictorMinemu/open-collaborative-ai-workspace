## Objective

Per D-22 … D-25, D-28 … D-30, D-32, PD-03, PD-15 … PD-17, P7 (hub `07.13-01-PLAN.md`). The Control Plane custody of
members' Work Zone sign-ins: sealed per member × site in the Work Zone scope, owner-only, revocable, expiring, loaded
only under the exact conditions of D-24 and D-32, and re-sealed when the device unloads them.

seal/open/list/revoke, load decision, upload and reseal routes, sweep, integration tests with tenant and owner
isolation.
