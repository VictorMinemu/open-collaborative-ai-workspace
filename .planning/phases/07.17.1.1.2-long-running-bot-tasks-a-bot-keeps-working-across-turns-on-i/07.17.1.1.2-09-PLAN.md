## Phase Goal

See `07.17.1.1.2-01-PLAN.md` (PD-05, PD-06, PD-09, PD-15, PD-18). **This plan's slice (the phase's API end to end,
continuation):** the platform delivers the bot's next turn by itself: `bot_task.continue` in three transactions, the
turn-done release, the minute tick, budgets, the nudge, and every safety stop.

<objective>
Create `agent-channel/task-wakes.ts` (a structural copy of 07.16's `work-session-wakes.ts`: derived event id, decide →
deliver as the requester → confirm or release), the turn-done hook in `chains.ts`, the `bot_task.tick`, the budget and
safety stops and the D-12 nudge; register them; prove single delivery, timers, lost turns, budgets, access loss and
sticky taint (D-02, D-03, D-04, D-07, D-08, D-12).

Purpose: this is what makes the bot keep working without a person re-prompting.
Output: one module, the done hook, the registration, four test files, helpers.
</objective>
