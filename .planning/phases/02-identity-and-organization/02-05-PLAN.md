## Phase Goal

**As an** Organization Admin and every member of the workspace, **I want to** arrange the company into departments, teams and projects with the four PRD roles, have every route decided by one permission resolver, and manage my own password, sessions and profile, **so that** each person and agent can do exactly what their role, units and rules allow and every sensitive change is attributed and audited.

**This plan's slice:** devices and the Work Zone (research inventory R9–R11, M16–M20, M22–M24) are decided by the resolver, including the Tool and Device layers.

<objective>
Migrate the device module's REST routes and the whole work-session module to the permission service (ORG-03): approvals use the creator / device-owner relations plus the admin role and the Tool layer, session creation intersects `work_session.create` with `device.use` (Device layer), lists are filtered per row, the `ws` topic authorizer uses `work_session.read`, and the device and session views carry `viewerCan` so the web stops recomputing policy. Prompts and session audit rows inherit the real client kind (AUTH-05). The Device Link socket (`/runtime/ws`, M21) is 02-11's.

Purpose: SC3 lists devices and Work Zone among the Phase 1 routes that must use the single resolver.
Output: device/routes.ts and work-session/{routes,index}.ts on perms, canUseDevice / listUsableProjects / isAdmin / assertCanApprove deleted, viewerCan in the web.
</objective>
