## Phase Goal

See `07.10-01-PLAN.md` (phase goal, plan map, PD-01…PD-14). **This plan's slice:** every session row says, for the
person who reads it, whether its team project may be named to them and what in it waits on them, and nothing per
viewer ever rides a shared stream. It closes the 07.9 D-14 leak the UI-SPEC asked to test first (R2, PD-02).

<objective>
Contracts first (C-02): `WorkSession.teamProjectHidden` and `WorkSession.attention` (R2). The API names a session's team
project only to people who may open it or who created the session (PD-04), and computes `attention` per viewer
(PD-05), on the list, the detail and each `user:` frame, while the `ws:` snapshot becomes viewer-neutral (PD-03). The
leak is proven RED first (D-14 of 07.9, C-01), live surfaces are covered by the extended sentinel, organization B is
covered, and the 07.9 list budget holds (D-09).

Purpose: WZS-05 (the data for Needs you), WZS-06 (the data that sends hidden-project sessions to Personal), WZS-09,
WZS-10.
Output: `viewer-fields.ts` (+ integration test), additive `work-session.ts` (+ tests), the shaping in `service.ts` and
`routes.ts`, the extended sentinel, `mergeSessionSnapshot` in the web and its use in the session view's data hook.
</objective>
