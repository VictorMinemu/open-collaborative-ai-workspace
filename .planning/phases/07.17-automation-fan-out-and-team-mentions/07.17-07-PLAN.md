## Phase Goal

See `07.17-01-PLAN.md` (PD-13). **This plan's slice:** `@team` becomes a real mention on channel posts, for people and bots,
under the resolver and the bot cap, without ever reaching the cell or waking a bot. 12 turns it into notifications; 13
into the composer and chips.

<objective>
Switch the message mention type to `MessageMention`, add team and department units to the mention directory, resolve unit
mentions only on channel posts with the author's (or the turn human's) `unit.read`, cap bots at three teams per message,
filter units out of the cell event, and prove precedence, scope, the cap and no bot wake (D-07, D-17, D-18, PD-13,
PD-25).

Purpose: AFO-04's "people and bots can mention a team" with ADR-0027 intact.
Output: contract and schema typing, directory and resolver changes at every write path, one integration file, unit tests.
</objective>

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-07.17-07-01 | Elevation of privilege | mentioning a team one cannot see | medium | mitigate | `unit.read` decided by the resolver for the author or the turn's human; test |
| T-07.17-07-02 | Spoofing | a unit handle shadowing a person or bot | medium | mitigate | Precedence agents → users → units; shadowed handles never offered (13); test |
