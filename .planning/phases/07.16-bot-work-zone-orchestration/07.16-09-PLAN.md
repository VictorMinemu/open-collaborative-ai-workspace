## Phase Goal

See `07.16-01-PLAN.md` (PD-16, PD-17). **This plan's slice:** the cell receives the wake-up as a turn the model can act on
and every turn of a bot with an active profile tells the model what it may start, where and how.

<objective>
Handle `agent.work_session.v1` in the bridge like `agent.consult_reply.v1`, map it to a turn with the Work Zone session
update block, and render `turn.workZone` as the "Your Work Zone" context block on every mapped turn (D-05, D-09, D-10,
D-11, PD-16, PD-17).

Purpose: without the block the model does not know its projects, models or modes; without the event the bot would have to
poll.
Output: bridge case, two mapper additions, one new bridge test file, mapper tests.
</objective>
