## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| browser → automation routes | Untrusted create/update bodies decide what a bot will do later without a person present |
| admin → others' automations | Governance actions on automations that run as someone else |

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-07.6-05-01 | Elevation of privilege | creating or restarting an automation that runs as another person | high | mitigate | run-as = caller always (and DB CHECK from 02); managers get 403 on edit, resume and run (Task 4) |
| T-07.6-05-02 | Elevation of privilege | targeting a channel the creator or bot cannot use | high | mitigate | `decideTarget` (agent membership, post, agent ∩ human read, personal-bot rule, bot.read) at create/update (Task 3) and again at every fire (09) |
| T-07.6-05-03 | Denial of service | many or very frequent automations | medium | mitigate | per-user cap, minimum interval, runs-per-day cap (Tasks 1, 4) |
| T-07.6-05-04 | Information disclosure | other members or tenants reading automations | high | mitigate | owner-only reads, `automation.manage` for `scope=all`, 404 across tenants (Task 4) |
| T-07.6-05-05 | Repudiation | unaudited governance actions | medium | mitigate | audit on every mutation with actor and ids (Tasks 1, 4) |
</threat_model>
