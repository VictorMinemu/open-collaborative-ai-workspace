See `07.17.1.1-01-PLAN.md` (PD-04, PD-21, PD-26). **This plan's slice:** the bots that may orchestrate their sessions
are now offered the two file tools with the four, and told their file limit; the routes of 12 and 13 get their shared helpers.

<objective>
Extend `WORK_SESSION_TOOL_NAMES` and `WORK_SESSION_TOOL_FLAGS` to six, put the applied file limit on
`TurnWorkZone.files`, keep the offer all-or-none for six, add the fake cell's keys, and move the Work Zone routes'
claim, refusal and audit helpers into `work-session-calls.ts` (D-01, D-07).

Purpose: 12's and 13's routes require the tools on the turn (`not_in_turn` otherwise) and reuse the helpers; the bot reads its
limit before trying.
Output: a contract change, the offer field, the six-slot proof, a pure refactor.
</objective>
