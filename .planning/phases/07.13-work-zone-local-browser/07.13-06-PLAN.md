## Objective

Per D-05, D-06, D-10, PD-18, PD-19, PD-27 (hub `07.13-01-PLAN.md`). The device side of delivery and consent: the pinned
headless shell is fetched and verified by the runtime itself, the device owner opts in locally, and doctor tells the
owner exactly what is missing. `07.13-SPIKES.md` (04) gives the measured apt package list per distro.

Purpose: the browser exists on any device without a manual download, and only where its owner allowed it. Output:
pins, installer, config section, `browser` CLI, doctor line, tests.
