## Decisions Made

See `key-decisions`. The test server for the 413 case discards the rest of the body, as nginx's lingering close does.
A first version reset the socket right away. That raced the response on loopback and failed once out of several runs.
The real-world behaviour (nginx lingering close, Go's `closeWriteAndWait` in Caddy) gives the client time to read the
answer.
