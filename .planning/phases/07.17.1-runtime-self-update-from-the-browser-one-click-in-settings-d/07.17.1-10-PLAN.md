the daemon takes the click, updates itself without cutting a turn, hands over to its service manager and the next daemon

D-05, D-06, D-07).

Purpose: everything below this plan is tested in isolation; this proves the whole device side over a real Device Link.
Output: daemon wiring, the CLI hook, daemon and CLI tests.
</objective>
