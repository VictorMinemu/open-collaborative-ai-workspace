<objective>
Per D-02, D-03, D-04, D-05, D-06, D-09, D-11, D-12, D-13, D-14, D-15. A member takes over a session that runs on a
shared server, in one locked transaction that moves `created_by` (so every owner rule moves at once, PD-01), never cuts
a running turn, cancels the queued prompts of people who lose access and tells everyone live. People rename a session
by hand. The API decides everything; the web (plans 06, 07) only renders `assign` and the preview.

Purpose: WZO-03, WZO-04, WZO-05 on the server. Output: `assign.ts`, `rename.ts`, the reauthorize option, two DTO
fields, integration tests on real PostgreSQL.

Discretion recorded (planner, under the owner's autonomy grant):
- The dry-run preview (`GET …/assign`) runs the same pure transfer computation the POST uses (`computeTransfer`:
  the UPDATE plus reads only) inside `withSavepoint(tx, fn, () => false)` from `packages/db/src/tenant.ts`, which
  always discards the savepoint and its buffered after-commit callbacks, so the confirm dialog (D-13, D-15) shows
  exactly what the POST will do and nothing else happens. All side effects (audit, events, emits, commands,
  cancellations, deliveries) live only in the POST, after `computeTransfer`.
- `assign` on the detail is the cheap decision (no savepoint); the list carries only the viewer-neutral
  `deviceHostKind` (no per-row permission check, `list-perf.int.test.ts` budgets unchanged).
- Manual rename uses `work_session.configure` (A2) and the typed-title rule 1–200 (A3).
