# Phase 07.17.1.1.2 Plan 08: The task routes and Stop Summary

**A bot can now start, continue and finish its own long task through three cell routes, and a person can stop it from `POST /api/v1/bot-tasks/:id/stop`. The routes run on a locked task row and keep a live platform-owned task card in the thread up to date. Budgets, the policy and the organization's running cap are checked on every call, every change is audited with ids and counts only, and other organizations cannot reach any of it.**

## Intended change (CLAUDE.md plan mode: permissions, audit, tenant data)

- **Cell routes.** `POST /agent-channel/v1/tasks/start|continue|finish` decide everything from the stored turn and the task row under `FOR UPDATE`. The body only names the turn, the bot and the bot's own text.
  - Order: tx0 without a user finds the turn (404 `unknown_turn`, audited in the cell's organization). tx1 runs as the turn's person: the live turn, then the tool on the turn, then the task's checks, then the claim, then the state change, card, job and audit.
- **Start.** Runs under the organization's advisory lock: no active task in the thread (`task_active`), the policy re-decided (`policy_off`), then the running cap (`org_cap`; 0 means no task may run).
- **Continue.** Policy off, every turn used and the deadline passed each stop the task with one message. A `waitFor` that does not belong to this task is `wait_unknown`.
- **Stop.** 404 `task_not_found` unless the caller reads the conversation (request-bound resolver, RLS). Then `mayStopTask`: the requester, a moderator (`message.delete_any` on the channel), or the bot's manager (`bot.configure` for a personal bot, `bot.manage` / `bot.manage_unit` for a team bot). Anyone else gets 403 `not_task_owner`.
- No new permission, grain or RLS change; no check was loosened.
- Audits: `bot.task_started`, `bot.task_finished`, `bot.task_stopped` and `bot.task_refused` carry only `{ taskId, conversationId, eventId?, turnsUsed?, maxTurns?, waitKind?, outcome?, byUserId?, code?, tool? }`.

## What was built

- **`tasks.ts`**, the state machine:
  - `lockTask`, `taskOfTurn`.
  - `startTask`: the advisory lock, the thread check, the policy, the cap, the insert (`ON CONFLICT DO NOTHING` maps to `task_active`), the ledger link, the card, the requester's follow and the audit.
  - `continueTask`: the PD-05 waits capped at `deadline_at`, `taskQuiet` only on platform-delivered turns, the `bot_task.continue` job at `wake_at` with no singleton key.
  - `finishTask` and `stopTask`, both through one idempotent `endTask`, plus the after-commit message.
  - `consumeWait` for 09, 11 and 12.
  - `mayStopTask`, the one stop rule (10 uses it for `viewerCan.stop`).
  - `auditTask`, `auditTaskRefused`, `taskSessionOf`.
- **`task-card.ts`**: `composeTaskCard` (pure: goal as the title, Progress and Next step fields, pill word, generic status, the stop notice), `postTaskCard` (asker's thread, `task:<id>`, `cardPlatformOwned`, a Follow action), `refreshTaskCard` (`updatePlatformCardNotified`, so followers hear status changes) and `taskMaxHours`.
- **`task-routes.ts`**: `registerTaskRoutes` and `registerTaskUserRoutes`. Both are registered in `index.ts`, the Stop route next to the chain Stop.
- **`claim.ts`**: a new `uncapped` option.
- **`testing-tasks.ts`**: the shared helpers for 09, 11 and 12.

## Deviations from Plan

### Auto-fixed Issues

**1. [Rule 1 - Bug] `countCall: false` still enforced the cap of 8 on task tools**
- **Found during:** Task 1, reading `claimToolCall`.
- **Issue:** The conditional UPDATE always checks `tool_calls < 8`. A turn that had used its 8 calls would have been refused `ocw_task_continue`, which contradicts D-13 and E17.
- **Fix:** A new claim option `uncapped` (only for non-side-effecting flags): the cap is neither checked nor counted. The default path is unchanged. A test now shows that a turn at 8 calls still continues and stays at 8.
- **Files modified:** `claim.ts`. **Commit:** c1c7c693

**2. [Rule 2 - Correctness] AI chat turns refused at the route (D-10)**
- An AI chat turn now answers `not_in_turn` even if its ledger listed a task tool. This sits on top of 07's offer. Test: "an AI chat turn never starts a task, even with the tool on its ledger".
- **Commit:** 8e062f4c

**3. [Rule 3 - Blocking] The strict authz guard needs a request-bound decision**
- **Issue:** The first Stop implementation used `isReadable`, and the strict guard answered 500 ("answered 200 without a permission decision").
- **Fix:** Readability now goes through `createConversationAccess(core).readable(req, …)`, the same `channel.read` decision the card routes make. Its 404 is re-coded `task_not_found`.
- **Commit:** 8e062f4c

**4. [Rule 3 - Blocking] The session wait is read in tx0**
- **Issue:** `work_sessions.audience_backstop` hides a bot session from a requester outside its audience.
- **Fix:** The continue route reads the awaited session without a user in tx0 (`taskSessionOf`, the pattern the Work Zone routes use) and passes the fact into `continueTask`.
- **Commit:** eb7d6c1c

**5. Smaller choices (no behaviour asked for by the plan was dropped)**
- `registerTaskRoutes(scope, core)` takes no `deps`: the cell routes need no `perms`.
- `mayStopTask` runs before the finished-task shortcut on Stop.
- `at` is null for `after_turn`.
- Continue calls are audited by the claim's `bot.tool_call`.
- The suites use the sandbox fixture's people (Sam = the plan's Ana, Alex = Ben).
- The personal-bot owner case calls `mayStopTask` directly on a seeded row: a personal bot lives only in its owner's DM, so its owner is always the requester there.
