# Phase 07.17.1.1.2 Plan 13: The browser journey of a long task Summary

**The fake cell now runs the three task tools the way the plugin does: it sends the same bodies, posts nothing on quiet turns and reports done for every task turn. On top of it, `bot-tasks.spec.ts` follows the owner's scenario in a real browser on desktop and mobile. Settings sets 4 turns per task. The bot then works through four turns by itself on one live card, a colleague writes in the thread without pausing it, and the budget stops it with one mention. A second task is stopped from the card. The ended card now hides "Next step".**

## What was built

- **UI fix (orchestrator request).** `TaskBody` reads Next step only while the task is active. A done, blocked, stopped or failed card keeps its last progress note and has no Next step. The web test covers all four endings and both active states. The e2e checks it on the budget-stopped card and the person-stopped card.
- **Fake cell (`fake-cell-plugin.ts`).**
  - **Task tools.** `ocw_task_start|continue|finish` go through `taskCall` to `POST /agent-channel/v1/tasks/<verb>`. Each sends the turn's `eventId` and `agentId` plus only that tool's own keys: strings trimmed, `null` dropped, unknown keys never sent. A tool that already succeeded in a turn is refused locally (`once_per_turn`) and nothing is sent.
  - **Quiet and done.** `TaskTurnOutcome` mirrors the plugin's `ActiveTurn.task`: a finish clears quiet. A quiet turn posts nothing. With `autoDone`, every turn offered a task tool and every `agent.task.v1` reports `/turns/:eventId/done`. The outcome is `replied` when the turn posted, continued or finished, and `failed` otherwise. Chain turns behave as before.
  - **Task turns.** An `agent.task.v1` is answered on the card, in the task's thread, through `taskCallsFor(event, { turn, maxTurns })`. The turn numbers come from `turn.task`, or else from the server's sentence.
  - **Records.** `taskRequests`, `taskTurns` and `taskOutcome(eventId)`.
- **`bot-tasks.ts`.** `readPack({ of, finishAt?, afterSeconds? })` continues with "Read n of N" and finishes at `finishAt`. `taskDirectives(goal, …)` builds the start turn, and the file holds the card selectors.
- **`bot-tasks.spec.ts`.** Three serial tests per project:
  1. **Settings › Bot policy.** The test checks the Long tasks row and its three labelled fields; on phones the fields are 44 px. An invalid value shows its sentence and turns Save off. The test saves 4, reloads, checks the 4 is still there and runs axe in light and dark.
  2. **Four turns by itself.**
     - **The card.** One `expect.poll` records every value the card shows. It sees Turn 1 → 2 → 3 → 4 of 4 and progress "Read 1/2/3 of 4" on one card (one message id) until Stopped. The notice reads "Stopped: it used its 4 turns.", Next step and Stop are gone, and the progress stays "Read 3 of 4".
     - **Ben.** Ben posts a thread reply, without a mention, as soon as Turn 2 shows. The task still reaches turn 4.
     - **The cell's records.** Turns 2–3 posted nothing and turn 4 says `refused (budget_turns)`. There was exactly one mention of the bot in the thread and three platform turns.
     - **The browser.** Exactly one bot message mentions `@ana-tasks`, and it is the stop sentence. There is one visible "continued" message, the card does not overflow, and axe passes in light and dark.
  3. **Stop from the card.** A task waits on a 600 s timer ("Waiting until …", Next step "Read 2 of 4").
     - **Ana.** Stop is named "Stop task: <goal>" and is 44 px tall on phones. axe passes with 44 px targets.
     - **Ben.** On the same thread he sees the card without Stop.
     - **After Ana presses Stop.** Both views turn Stopped with "Stopped by Ana Gil.", without Next step, keeping progress "Read 1 of 4". After 12 s no `agent.task.v1` has arrived for that task and the card still says Turn 1.
  - **Setup.** The org's limits are set to 40 / 8 / 100 before the tests and restored afterwards. The spec's bot is deleted. Test passwords are built at runtime, the expected sentences come from `@ocw/contracts`, and the file contains no bidi characters.
