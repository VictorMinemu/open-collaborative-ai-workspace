## Top 3 Priority Fixes

1. **WARNING: The team pill and the reserved "+" slot crush project names.** In `sidebar-full-1440`, "Customer portal" shows as "Custome…" and "Ledger lab" as "Ledger lab 14…", while the pill keeps "Web 1440-m…". At 390 the same happens ("Customer portal 3…"). The name is the row's primary information (S3), but it currently loses to secondary meta.
   - **Cause:** in `rows.tsx:24` the pill is `shrink-0 max-w-[40%]`. On top of that, `rows.tsx:85-88` reserves an always-present `w-6` spacer when `plus && live <= 0`.
   - **Fix:** make the pill shrinkable (`shrink min-w-0 max-w-[35%]`) and let the name keep a minimum width (`min-w-[5ch]` or `basis-1/2`). Drop the spacer: hide the pill on `group-hover/project` / focus with `pointer-fine`, and put the "+" in its slot. This keeps the "no layout shift" rule without stealing 24 px from every idle row.
2. **WARNING: The needs-you approval reason leaves almost no room for the title.** At 1440, "Export the i…" shows only 12 characters next to "✋ Needs approval" (~105 px). This is the most important row in the sidebar, and its title is the least readable part.
   - **Cause:** `needs-you-section.tsx:76-87` (reason span `shrink-0`) and the `REASON.approval` text and icon at `needs-you-section.tsx:89-94`.
   - **Fix:** in the needs-you row only, show the `Hand` icon plus "Approval" (the full "Needs approval" stays in `aria-label` and `title`). Or let the reason drop its text below a width with `@container` and keep the icon. Changing the visible text needs a one-line amendment to the Copywriting Contract.
3. **WARNING: Some text and row sizes are outside the contract.** The contract allows only 11.5, 12 and 13.5 px.
   - `work-zone-sidebar.tsx:211` renders "No sessions yet." at `text-[13px]`; the contract says 12/400.
   - `settled-section.tsx:48` renders the Settled more row at `h-8 text-[13px]`, while every other more row is `h-7 text-[12px]` (`rows.tsx:205`, `needs-you-section.tsx:148`).
   - **Fix:** change both to `text-[12px]`, and change the Settled more row to `h-7` (keep `max-md:h-11`). Better still, reuse the shared `ExpandRow` from `needs-you-section.tsx:126-150` so the three kinds of more row can't drift apart.

## Detailed Findings

### Pillar 1: Copywriting (3/4)
- PASS: these strings match the contract exactly:
  - "Couldn't load projects." / "Couldn't load sessions." + "Try again" (`sections.tsx:360,370`, `work-zone-sidebar.tsx:189,199`)
  - "Nothing matches “{q}”." (`search-results.tsx:116`)
  - The onboarding pair (`onboarding-line.tsx:15,27`)
  - "Show {n} more" / "Show fewer" / "Show all {n}"
  - Singular "more project" / "more repository" (`sections.tsx:225,287`)
  - The aria label "{n} more active session(s) in {project}" (`rows.tsx:221`)
  - Tooltip "New project in {unit}" (seen in `sidebar-hover-plus-1440`)
- WARNING: "Needs approval" plus its icon takes about 45 % of the needs-you row at 1440 (see Fix 2). The copy is correct, but its length doesn't fit the slot.
- WARNING (minor): in the Personal section, the "+{n} more" label (`sections.tsx:274`) expands inline. Under projects, the same "+{n} more" label navigates (`rows.tsx:224`). The same words do two different things. The contract allows it, but "Show {n} more" in Personal would match the other inline expanders.

### Pillar 2: Visuals (2/4)
- WARNING: names lose the width to meta (Fix 1). Seen in `sidebar-full-1440`, `sidebar-hover-plus-1440` ("Custome…", "Mobile app 14…") and `sidebar-full-390-dark`.
- WARNING: the needs-you titles are unreadable at 1440 (Fix 2).
- WARNING (minor): the position of the count pill in collapsed headers is inconsistent (`sidebar-collapsed-1440-dark`). Department pills sit at the right edge, because the truncated title fills the row. "PERSONAL 2" sits right after the title. The cause is `sidebar-section-header.tsx:70` (`ml-1`). This inherits the chat behaviour (S1), but with mixed title lengths the column looks ragged. Option: `ml-auto` in both sidebars. That would be a chat DOM change, so R8 requires a chat check.
- PASS: the hierarchy reads well:
  - Uppercase muted section labels, 13.5 px rows, 12 px meta.
  - Sub-rows indent under the project name (the 2 px optical offset is acceptable).
  - The idle hollow dot and the running filled dot are clearly different.
  - Every icon-only "+" has an aria-label and a tooltip.

### Pillar 3: Color (3/4)
- PASS: the accent appears only on:
  - The live dot (`rows.tsx:38`).
  - The running glyph (`rows.tsx:130`).
  - The `text-primary` tone for "Working" (`rows.tsx:153`, `needs-you-section.tsx:30`).
  - The `ProjectBadge` (`session-rows.tsx:34`).
  - Focus rings.
- PASS: amber appears only on the approval glyph and text, and it reads well in both light and dark (`sidebar-full-390-dark`). "Your turn" uses foreground with no colour, as the contract says.
- WARNING: the "Try again" link buttons (`sections.tsx:368`, `work-zone-sidebar.tsx:197`, `variant="link"`) render in primary (`sidebar-error-1440`). The contract's exhaustive accent list includes only the onboarding "Create project" link. Either add the error links to the list in the spec, or style them as `text-foreground underline`.
- No hardcoded hex or rgb values in the sidebar files.

### Pillar 4: Typography (3/4)
- Sizes in use: 8 (ProjectBadge, inherited), 10.5 (pills, inherited), 11.5, 12, 13, 13.5.
- Weights in use: 400, 500 (inherited), 600, 700 (badge, inherited).
- WARNING: the two 13 px uses are not in the contract: `work-zone-sidebar.tsx:211` and `settled-section.tsx:48` (Fix 3).
- PASS: the section header is 11.5/600 uppercase `tracking-wider` (`sidebar-section-header.tsx:64`). The search headers match it (`search-results.tsx:48`). The search field is `max-md:text-base` (`work-zone-sidebar.tsx:165`, R9 done).

### Pillar 5: Spacing (3/4)
- WARNING: the always-on `w-6` spacer in `rows.tsx:85-88` (Fix 1).
- WARNING: the Settled more row is `h-8 px-2.5`, while the other more rows are `h-7` (Fix 3).
- PASS: these match the contract:
  - Sub-rows `h-7 pl-8 pr-2.5 gap-2` (`rows.tsx:183`).
  - Header `h-7` with `max-md:h-11` (`sidebar-section-header.tsx:44,54`).
  - Section "+" `size-6` with `max-md:size-11` and `[@media(hover:none)]:opacity-100` (`sections.tsx:172`).
  - Repository "+" visible on touch (`rows.tsx:305`).
  - Project "+" `pointer-coarse:hidden` (`rows.tsx:115`).
  - Skeleton rows `h-8`, `max-md:h-11` (`sections.tsx:301-305`).
- The only arbitrary values are inherited ones (`rounded-[4px]`, `max-w-[40%]`, `ring-[3px]`).

### Pillar 6: Experience Design (3/4)

  - Loading skeleton (`sections.tsx:300-306`).
  - Projects-failed line with the fallback grouping (`sidebar-error-1440`: the project row, its Working session and Personal all still render).
  - Sessions-failed line (`work-zone-sidebar.tsx:187-200`).
  - Onboarding.
  - Search results with a polite live region (`search-results.tsx:112`).
  - Hidden-project capture.
  - Collapsed headers with count pills, and Settled with no pill.
- PASS: the hover "+" replaces the live count in place (`rows.tsx:89-97`). The project row shows a hover state while its "+" is visible (`sidebar-hover-plus-1440`).
- WARNING: I can't confirm the muted style for non-member search hits from `sidebar-search-1440`. Make sure the scenario has a non-member hit and assert `text-muted-foreground` on it in the e2e spec.

The "+2 more" and kept-row visuals are covered only in part.
