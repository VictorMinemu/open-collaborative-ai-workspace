| T-07.16-19-02 | Tampering | injected prompts into a running session | high | mitigate | Tainted prompts refused without the flag; marked tainted and visible with it |
| T-07.16-19-03 | Spoofing | a proposal looking trustworthy | medium | mitigate | Card warning notice; the person reviews the exact prompt; Start marks the prompt |
| T-07.16-19-04 | Repudiation | actions after outside content | medium | mitigate | `outsideContent: true` on every audit row of these paths |
| T-07.16-19-05 | Elevation of privilege | weakening the existing proposal gate | high | mitigate | `ocw_start_work_zone` still refuses tainted turns (test); only the start route's fallback creates tainted proposals |
