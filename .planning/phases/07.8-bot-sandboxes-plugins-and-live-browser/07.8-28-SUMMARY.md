# Phase 07.8 Plan 28: Security sweep Summary

**The phase's isolation and privacy hold end to end with a real Chromium: Sam signs in for a bot by typing in the real
page through the live view, nothing he typed exists anywhere but the site, nobody else sees a frame while he controls the
page or while his session is loaded, and every way the bot tries to carry what it saw to another host stops. Every
sandbox, browser, session and live-view route refuses another organization from the authz registry. A bot's member data
now stays with its conversation, a deleted bot's sessions go with it, dead takeover leases are swept, a cookie set
before any sign-in is no longer sealed as one, and a crashed sandboxd leaves nothing behind.**

## Decisions to record in ADR-0029

1. **Sticky member data (#292, coordinator decision).** Once a member's session was loaded by a bot in a conversation
   (`app.agent_member_data`), every later turn of that bot there has member data: P-11 refusals on non-personal bots,
   D-13 approval before another site or an egress exec, download routing to the conversation, import refusal. The pair
   is per conversation, whatever the thread. Its `sites` are the hosts a later turn may open without approval; with
   none known every host asks. A page opened without a session in such a turn is site-locked to its host. Closing a
   signed-in page reads nothing and marks nothing. The pair ends with the bot or the conversation (FK cascade); no
   product action resets a conversation's history yet. Existing turns with `member_data` were backfilled.
2. **A sign-in is a hand back after a change (#284).** The site's state at `take` is fingerprinted in memory; an
   unchanged state at hand back seals nothing. Without a baseline (its export failed), anything of the site is kept, as
   before. A site whose pre-login state changes during the member's control (a consent click) still seals: the explicit
   hand back is the member's statement, and the session is listed and revocable.
3. **Lock failure is visible and fail-closed (#284):** `crashed` with `lockFailed` audited; the page is closed in
   sandboxd after its row ended; the session stays sealed and loads only into a page locked from its start.
4. **A deleted bot takes its sessions (#287)** at the soft delete, through a tenant-scoped, deleted-bots-only SECURITY
   DEFINER purge.
5. **Dead leases (#271)** are swept by the shared browser sweep one minute past expiry (the holder's own live view
   keeps the first minute).
6. **Secret flags are masked in every approval view (#294)**, display only.
7. **Package-run leftovers (#260)** are swept at sandboxd boot by labels and run name prefix.
8. **The exact D-13 exec card** in the requester's own one-human DM shows the command (07.5 ADR-0026); it is the only
   place outside the stored arguments where the command's data appears (leak-sweep.int asserts exactly that).
9. **E2E fake sites topology:** their own internal network that only the egress proxy joins; sandboxd's outbound
   network keeps inter-container traffic off.
