## Phase Goal

See `07.9-01-PLAN.md`. **This plan's slice:** the audience becomes changeable and readable through the API — one PUT
that applies at once and revokes live views, one view that names exactly who can see the session now — and new
sessions start with the right audience, including those started from a bot's proposal.
