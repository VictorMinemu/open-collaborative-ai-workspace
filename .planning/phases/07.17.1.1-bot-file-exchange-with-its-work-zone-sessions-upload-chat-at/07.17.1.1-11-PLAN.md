## Phase Goal

See `07.17.1.1-01-PLAN.md` (PD-06 … PD-08, PD-12, PD-13, PD-15 … PD-18). **This plan's slice:** the work-session
module decides and runs one file transfer for a bot's own session: the session's state, the sponsor's rights, the
budgets under the bot's lock, the command, the wait and the outcome.

<objective>
Add `waitForFileAck` and the file-kind ack publish to the device hub, `createBotFiles` (`limitFor`, `prepareUpload`,
`prepareDownload`, `deadlineFor`, `waitForAck`, `finish`) to `work-session/bot-files.ts` exposed as
`WorkSessionModule.botFiles`, the test-only deadline option, and the two audit actions to `auditBotWorkSession` (D-02 …
D-07, D-13).

Purpose: 12's and 13's routes call exactly these methods (`limitFor`, `prepare*` and `finish` inside their transactions,
`deadlineFor` and `waitForAck` between them).
Output: one service, the hub additions, the audit builder, one integration file.
</objective>
