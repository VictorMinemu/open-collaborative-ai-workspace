## Phase Goal

See `07.17-01-PLAN.md` (PD-09, PD-10). **This plan's slice:** every opened item gets its own bot turn in its own thread, with
the item as outside content and the 07.16 Work Zone tools, so the bot reads, starts a session, moves the card and mentions
the team, per order (success criteria 2 and 3).

<objective>

trigger: unattended, tainted, Work Zone offer), skip with reasons when it can no longer run, keep it idempotent, and prove
the whole per-item API journey across 07.16, 08 and 12 (D-04, D-06, D-07, D-09, D-12, PD-09, PD-10).

Purpose: AFO-02's "each new conversation gets its own bot turn … including the Work Zone profile of 07.16" and AFO-03 end
to end at the API.
Output: one agent-channel delivery file, the worker, the trigger and offer changes, one integration file.
</objective>
