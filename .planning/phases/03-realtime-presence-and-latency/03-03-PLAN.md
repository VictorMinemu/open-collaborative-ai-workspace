## Phase Goal

**As a** member collaborating in a channel or a Work Zone session, **I want to** see who else is here right now (active or away), get my colleagues' messages, typing and presence within half a second, and stop receiving a conversation the moment I lose access to it, **so that** shared conversations feel live and never keep streaming to someone who was removed.

**This plan's slice (RT-04, surfaces):** presence where people look for it: the channel member list and the Work Zone session header (PRD §25 "[V] [A] [G] +2").

<objective>
Show the 03-01 presence in the two remaining surfaces of D-08: dots and present-first ordering in the channel members dialog, and a viewers stack in the Work Zone session header (desktop and mobile), each proven by a two-browser e2e and reviewed in light and dark at 1440 and 390.

Purpose: SC2 "Presence … appears per conversation" in every place the owner asked for (channel header done in 03-01).
Output: members-dialog.tsx and session-view.tsx changes, e2e assertions in presence.spec.ts and work-zone.spec.ts, screenshots.
</objective>
