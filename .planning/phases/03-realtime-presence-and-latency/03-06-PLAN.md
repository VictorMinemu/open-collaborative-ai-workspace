## Phase Goal

**As a** member collaborating in a channel or a Work Zone session, **I want to** see who else is here right now (active or away), get my colleagues' messages, typing and presence within half a second, and stop receiving a conversation the moment I lose access to it, **so that** shared conversations feel live and never keep streaming to someone who was removed.

**This plan's slice (RT-01 breadth, phase gate):** every kind of access change — role, rule, unit — re-decides live subscriptions, the Work Zone shows it, a lost message is caught by the backstop; then the ADR, the demo notes and the phase gate.

## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| organization mutations → live sockets of the whole organization | Role, rule and unit changes alter what many users may read |
| Redis pub/sub (at-most-once) → gateways | Lost messages must not leave access open indefinitely |

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-03-06-01 | Elevation of privilege | Work Zone stream after a role downgrade | high | mitigate | `member.updated` (role/status) invalidator → `ws` authorizer (resolver) → `revoked` within 1 s; integration and e2e tests |
| T-03-06-02 | Elevation of privilege | streams after a deny rule or unit change | high | mitigate | `permission_rules.updated` and `units.updated` invalidate the whole organization; integration test with an explicit deny |
| T-03-06-03 | Elevation of privilege | lost invalidation message | medium | mitigate | 5-minute recheck re-decides every live subscription; backstop test with a 200 ms interval |
| T-03-06-04 | Denial of service | organization-wide re-decisions | low | mitigate | 03-04 coalescing and concurrency limit; profile-only member updates trigger nothing |
| T-03-06-05 | Information disclosure | ADR / DEMO.md | low | accept | Documents design and test fixtures only; no secrets, hosts or credentials |
</threat_model>
