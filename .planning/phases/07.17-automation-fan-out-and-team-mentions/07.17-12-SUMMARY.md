# Phase 07.17 Plan 12: Team mention notifications Summary

**`@team` on a channel post now notifies the active members of the unit's subtree who can read the channel. Each gets one `team_mention` row with the unit's id, ranked below a direct mention and above `@channel`. Delivery is capped at 200 people per message (the first by user id) and announced like a mention. The inbox shows the unit (`null` once it is deleted). Edits, bot wakes and other organizations never get a notification.**

## Accomplishments

- **Audience** (`team-mentions.ts`). One grouped query covers each mentioned team or department, joins its subtree (`path @>`), the unit memberships and the active `org_members`, and excludes the author. Each person appears once, under the first unit in mention order, and the query stops at 200 people by user id. Tenant joins are explicit, with RLS as the backstop. Only ids are read: no name or handle is logged or audited (P1).
- **Recipients.** `computeRecipients` offers `team_mention` on channel posts, and the contract's rank decides each person's kind (direct `mention` > `team_mention` > `channel` / `here` / `thread_reply`).
- **Delivery.** `onPosted` expands unit mentions for channels only. AI chats keep their mention-only path. `deliver` stores `unit_id` for `team_mention` rows, maps `team_mention` to the `mention` notify event (popup) and adds `unit` to `notification.created`. `eligible` stays the only readability filter (P-03).
- **Inbox.** `team_mention` is in `MENTIONS_FILTER_KINDS`. `GET /notifications` resolves `unit` at read time. The mention count already counts `team_mention` through `MENTION_KINDS` (plan 01).
- **Edits.** `onEdited` is unchanged: it notifies newly mentioned people only, never units.
