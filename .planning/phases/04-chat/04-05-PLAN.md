## Phase Goal

**As a** member of a team that works in the workspace, **I want to** hold my whole daily conversation there — channels grouped by department and team, DMs and group DMs with people and bots, messages I can edit, delete, react to, pin, save and link to, @channel/@here and notifications I control, search and files — **so that** I do not need a second chat tool and every message stays attributed, editable under audit and delivered in realtime.

**This plan's slice (tracer):** a reaction toggled in one browser updates the count live in another (contracts → API → `reaction.updated` → web), then pins and personal saves on the same message-actions surface. Planner decisions P-01 … P-12 are in `04-01-PLAN.md` (P-03 readable set, P-04 events, P-05 tombstones, P-08 executor rules, P-09 UI).

Output: chat/reactions.ts, pins.ts, saves.ts; ReactionBar, EmojiPicker (frimousse), PinnedSheet, SavedView at `/chat/saved`; integration suites and `e2e/tests/reactions-pins.spec.ts`.
