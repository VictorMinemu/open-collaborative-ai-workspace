# Phase 07.17 Plan 04: Every few minutes, the daily-limit warning, the 5 / 288 limits doc Summary

**The automation editor now offers "Every few minutes" (5, 10, 15 or 30). Picking 10 saves `*/10 * * * *` and pre-fills 144 runs a day. Intervals below the workspace minimum are disabled and say why. An amber `role="status"` callout warns, before saving, when the bot's daily limit is lower than the turns the automation can use. The self-hosting doc states the limits as 5 minutes and 288 runs a day.**

- **Files modified:** 8

## Accomplishments

- **New preset.** "Every few minutes" is the first preset, with a "How often" select (`schedule-interval`, 44 px on phones). Each option's text carries its reason when disabled ("Every 5 minutes · Below your workspace's minimum (10 min)"). A stored `*/N` with N in 5/10/15/30 opens as the preset. `*/7`, `*/20` and step crons limited to some hours or days open as Custom. Every 07.16 preset round-trips as before.
- **Runs-a-day prefill.** When the person picks the preset or changes the interval, runs a day becomes `min(cap, floor(1440 / N))`. The field stays editable, and loading an automation never changes it.
- **No literal 15 left.** The frequency error, the editor's form message and `automationErrorText` use the preview's or the options' minimum. Their only fallback is `AUTOMATION_LIMITS.minScheduleMinutesDefault` (5).
- **Daily-limit callout.** `DailyLimitWarning` sits under runs a day. It shows "Scout can take 100 turns a day. This automation can use up to 144. Raise the bot's daily limit in its sheet, or runs stop when it is reached." It is non-blocking: nothing in `validate()` or the footer reads it. It is not shown within the limit, for a bot with no limit, or while runs a day is invalid.
- **Doc.** The `docs/self-hosting/automations.md` limits table now reads 5 minutes and 288, followed by the upgrade sentence.

## Decisions Made
