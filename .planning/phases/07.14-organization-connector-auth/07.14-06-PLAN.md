## Phase Goal

See `07.14-01-PLAN.md`. **This plan's slice (tracer, policy-consumer tier):** the three places outside the connector
proxy that ask "is this a member's account?" — per-tool approvals, agent templates and member-account shares — answer
with the shared predicate, so an organization account is treated as the organization resource it is (D-04).

<objective>
Convert the classification sites in `tool-approval/{kind,policies}.ts`, `agent/templates.ts` and `connectors/shares.ts`
(RESEARCH inventory #7, #16-#19) to the predicates of 01 and prove the organization-mode meaning with integration tests.

Purpose: an approval card that says "your account" for the organization's account, or a share of it, would be wrong
copy and a wrong audience promise.
Output: four converted files and their extended tests.
</objective>
