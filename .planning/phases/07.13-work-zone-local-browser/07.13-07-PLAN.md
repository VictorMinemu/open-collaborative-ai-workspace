## Objective

Per D-10, D-11, D-19, D-21, D-24, D-26, D-32, D-34, PD-09, PD-10, PD-20, PD-26 (hub `07.13-01-PLAN.md`). The server
decides the browser per session and per turn: org policy ∩ the device's opt-in ∩ its capability, the admins' per-tool
rule modes, whether a sign-in may load at all (human session, audience ⊆ {member, device owner}), and who may answer a
browser approval. The device receives the result with each turn and re-checks it on every call (09/12).

Purpose: principle 6 (least privilege, intersected) and ADR-0012's pattern for the browser. Output: policy field,
grant computation, DTO state, approval routing, integration tests with tenant isolation.
