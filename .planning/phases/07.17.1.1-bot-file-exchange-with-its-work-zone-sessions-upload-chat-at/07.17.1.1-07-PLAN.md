## Phase Goal

See `07.17.1.1-01-PLAN.md` (PD-05). **This plan's slice:** the device-authenticated, single-use read side of the byte
transport: a device fetches exactly the attachment its transfer names, once, under its own JWT.

<objective>
Extract the device JWT check (`device/auth.ts`), add the ledger primitives (`work-session/bot-files.ts`), the `GET`
transfer route with its per-process stream counter (`work-session/file-transfer-routes.ts`), and the FakeDevice helper
that performs a real fetch (D-02, D-08).

Purpose: 04's daemon fetches uploads from exactly this route; 08 adds the `PUT` beside it; 11 enqueues the commands.
Output: two new source files, four edited, one new integration file.
</objective>

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-07.17.1.1-07-01 | Spoofing | another device or organization using a transfer id | high | mitigate | JWT → device; the row must name that device; RLS by the device's organization; 404 cases |
| T-07.17.1.1-07-02 | Tampering | replayed or late transfers | high | mitigate | Single use (`pending` → `streaming` under `FOR UPDATE`), `expires_at`, 410 cases |
| T-07.17.1.1-07-03 | Denial of service | many concurrent streams | medium | mitigate | At most four streams per process, checked before the claim (503 keeps the row usable) |
| T-07.17.1.1-07-04 | Information disclosure | file names or paths in logs | medium | mitigate | Logs carry ids and codes; a captured-log assertion (P1) |
| T-07.17.1.1-07-05 | Elevation of privilege | legacy raw device tokens on a new route | medium | mitigate | JWT only; a 401 case |
</threat_model>
