## Phase Goal

**As an** Organization Admin and every member of the workspace, **I want to** arrange the company into departments, teams and projects with the four PRD roles, have every route decided by one permission resolver, and manage my own password, sessions and profile, **so that** each person and agent can do exactly what their role, units and rules allow and every sensitive change is attributed and audited.

**This plan's slice:** the structure half of the admin console (ORG-04 departments, teams, projects and memberships; ORG-01 including moves) as Settings › Organization.

<objective>
Build Settings › Organization on the 02-06 APIs (research §Admin console UX item 2, D-07, D-09): an indented tree with kind icons and member counts, create / rename / move / delete dialogs (no drag and drop: accessible and mobile-friendly), unit detail with members, Lead toggle, Add people and Remove, and a read-only access-rules card (D-02). Every control follows server permissions (`useCan`, `OrgUnit.viewerCan`). Hierarchy "projects" are org units of kind project; Work Zone repositories are not shown here (Pitfall 12).

Purpose: SC2's "Admin creates and rearranges Organization → Department → Team → Project (including moving units) … memberships are managed from CRUD pages in the admin console".
Output: settings entry + page, organization components, org-queries.ts, admin-org e2e spec, reviewed screenshots.
</objective>
