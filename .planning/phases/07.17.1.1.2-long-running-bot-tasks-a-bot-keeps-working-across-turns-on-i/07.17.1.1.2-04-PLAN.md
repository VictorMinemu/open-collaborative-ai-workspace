## Phase Goal

See `07.17.1.1.2-01-PLAN.md` (Cross-plan contracts › Policy, PD-09, PD-20, PD-27). **This plan's slice:** "Allow long
tasks" and the three limits, stored, read, decided per bot and shown in Settings and on the bot's capabilities.

<objective>
Add the `longTasks` built-in capability (bot toggle, default on; organization switch with its personal sub-switch) and
`BotPolicy.tasks` (max turns, max hours, max running tasks) to the contracts, the API policy and capability view, a
`loadTaskPolicy` helper and `computeTurn.longTasks`; show them in Settings › Bot policy and on the capabilities panel
(D-04).

Purpose: 07 gates the task tools on `computeTurn.longTasks`; 08 re-decides `loadTaskPolicy` at start and continue and
snapshots the limits.
Output: contract fields, policy load and save, the effective capability, two web surfaces with tests.
</objective>
