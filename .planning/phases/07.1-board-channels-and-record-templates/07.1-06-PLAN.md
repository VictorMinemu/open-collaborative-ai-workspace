## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| browser → card text edits | Untrusted titles, descriptions and field values; authorship decides |
| browser → column routes | Column names and moves from members; `channel.update` decides |
| card text → search index | What a reader can find must equal what they can read |

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-07.1-06-01 | Tampering | editing another person's card text | high | mitigate | Author-only text edits (`not_author`), Phase 4 rule kept; tests for member, admin and agent-authored cards |

| T-07.1-06-06 | Repudiation | who renamed or deleted a column, who edited a card | low | mitigate | `board.column.*` and `board.card.updated` audits; `message_versions` history |
