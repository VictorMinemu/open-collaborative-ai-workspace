# Phase 07.3 Plan 03: Module gating in the web Summary

When a module is off for a member, the web hides its entries and shows a calm in-place off state on deep links. This
covers AI chats, Work Zone and Bots, it happens live in an open tab, and the only input is `/me/permissions` plus the
catalog's entry grain. While permissions load, or after the fetch fails, nothing is hidden.

## What was built

- **`lib/modules.ts` (pure).**
  - `moduleStatus` and `grainStatus` return `loading`, `on` or `off`. The last decision wins. With no decision yet, a
    failure counts as `on`.
  - `permissionsFailed`: once a fetch has failed, it stays failed (see deviation 2).
  - `withoutBotDms`: drops only 1:1 agent DMs and keeps the order.
- **`components/shell/module-gate.tsx`.**
  - `useModule` and `useGrain`.
  - `ModuleGate` renders a skeleton (title bar plus three rows), the off state or the children. Its `keepOpen` key lets
    a page that explains its own lost access stay mounted.
  - `ModuleOff` uses `EmptyState` with the module's rail icon. The title is "<catalog label> is/are turned off for
    you", followed by the module's reassurance and one outline "Go to Chat". It carries `data-testid="module-off"` and
    `data-module`.
- **AI chats.**
  - The new `chat/ai/layout.tsx` and the share layout gate `/chat/ai`, `/chat/ai/new`, `/chat/ai/<id>` and
    `/share/ai/<id>`; the chat sidebar stays.
  - The sidebar's AI chats section and the AI Chats view leave only on `off`, in both the desktop rows and the 390
    segmented row. Test ids are unchanged and the other views keep their order.
- **Work Zone.**
  - The layout is a client component that gates every Work Zone URL. An open session keeps its own access-lost panel.
  - Settings › Devices keeps the device list, rename, sharing and revoke. "Add device", "Connect your first device" and
    "Add project from this computer" leave on `device.pair` off. When there is no device, a quiet "You have no paired
    devices." box takes their place.
- **Bots.**
  - `/bots` is gated.
  - A bot DM deep link shows the Bots off state: `DmView` became a thin wrapper around the old component, now
    `DmConversation`, and shows a skeleton while the decision loads. People and groups are unchanged.
  - Bot DMs leave the sidebar, its All count, the All view and the AI chats home (strip and list). The home's "Browse
    bots" links and its bot copy hide only on `off`.
- **Cross-links (Task 2 step 4).** A grep of `apps/web` found no links to `/work-zone`, `/bots` or `/chat/ai` outside
  their own sections, other than the nav, search (`search-palette.tsx`, `search-results.tsx`) and inbox (`inbox.tsx`).
  The nav was already gated; search and inbox are filtered by the API (07.3-01 gap 1). Nothing new was gated.

## Deviations from Plan

### Auto-fixed issues

**1. [Rule 1 - Bug, spec] Strict-mode locator and unread math in my own spec.**
- **Issue:** The bot DM locator matched both projects' bot DMs. The All count expected exactly one bot DM to leave, but
  the other project's bot DM is unread too.
- **Fix:** Rows are matched by `data-bot-name`. The expected drop is computed from Mia's unread bot DMs over the API.
- **Commits:** 2e49954, 28029f0.

**2. [Rule 1 - Bug] A persistently failing `/me/permissions` made gates loop between the skeleton and the page.**
- **Found during:** Task 2 (`/chat/ai` stayed on the skeleton for over 10 s in the failed-fetch case).
- **Cause:** TanStack Query resets a query that has no data to `pending` on each refetch (`fetchState` clears the
  error). Every newly mounted observer refetches an errored query, so `isError` flipped back and forth and the gate
  remounted its children forever. Task 1's failed-fetch test had passed only by catching a "visible" moment.
- **Fix:** `permissionsFailed({ isError, errorUpdateCount })` is sticky and has a unit test. The failed-fetch test now
  also asserts that the page is still there after 2 s, with no skeleton.
- **Commit:** 3ebba6f.

**3. [Rule 1 - Regression] The Work Zone gate replaced an open session's own "You no longer have access to this
session" panel.**
- **Found during:** Task 3. `work-zone.spec` RT-01 demotes Sam to `standard`, who has no `work_session.read`.
- **Fix:** `ModuleGate` takes a `keepOpen` key, and the Work Zone layout (now a client component) passes the open
  session's path. That session keeps its RT-01 panel until the member leaves it. "Back to Work Zone" then shows the off
  state. The home, other sessions and fresh deep links still swap live.
- **Effect on truth 4:** For a session that is already open, the live swap goes to the session's access-lost panel
  instead of `module-off`. The Work Zone home still swaps to `module-off` live, as the plan's behaviour list asks.
- **New e2e:** a session on Mia's own fake device is open when Work Zone goes off. The test checks the panel, then
  "Back to Work Zone" leading to `module-off`, then that Mia can still revoke the device.
- **Commit:** 7b8e7ef.

**4. [P-08 consistency] Device pairing uses `useGrain("device.pair") !== "off"` instead of `useCan("device.pair")`.**
- **Why:** `useCan` is false while loading and after a failure, which would hide pairing on a failed fetch, against
  P-08.
- **Also:** The pairing dialog opened by `?add=1` follows the same rule.

**5. [Additive] Extra screenshots and tests beyond the plan.**
- A `module-off-bots-dm-*` screenshot set, in addition to `module-off-bots-*`.
- A flash watcher, a MutationObserver installed per navigation, proves that each deep link never renders the page it
  guards. A fresh load of a module that is on never renders `module-off`.
