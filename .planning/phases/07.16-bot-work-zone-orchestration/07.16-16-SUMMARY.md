# Phase 07.16 Plan 16: The bot prompts and stops its own session; people keep control Summary

**A bot now sends follow-up prompts to the Work Zone session it started and stops it when the work is done. Both go through the queue and the archive people use, in the sponsor's name. The bot can touch only its own sessions, and people in the session's audience keep prompting and archiving it with their own rights. 18/18 new integration cases pass locally on real PostgreSQL and Redis.**

## Intended change (CLAUDE.md plan mode: permissions, audit, tenant data)

- Two new cell routes, `POST /agent-channel/v1/work-sessions/prompt` and `/stop`.
  - tx0 has no user. It finds the turn of this bot, its profile and its own session (`ownBotSession`, the one rule).
  - tx1 runs as the sponsor. It checks the live turn, then the tool on the turn, then ownership, then the profile status and taint (prompt only), then the claim, then `BotSessions.prompt` / `stop`.
- A prompt goes through `submitWorkPrompt` and a stop through `archiveWorkSession`. No check is copied. The prompt adds one resolver decision: the sponsor's own `work_session.prompt` on the session.
- No permission check, RLS policy or approval gate is weakened. Human routes are untouched. `interrupt` is not exposed to bots, and no route resolves a runtime approval (P3).
- Audits: `bot.work_session_prompted`, `bot.work_session_stopped` and `bot.work_session_refused`, all with ids, the outcome and `promptChars` only.

## Accomplishments

- **Tracer.** Atlas starts a session and its first turn completes. A second turn sends "now run the tests". The answer is `queued` at position 2. The row has `author_user_id` = Víctor (the sponsor) and `author_agent_id` = Atlas, and the device receives the prompt. A stop answers `stopped`. The session is then archived, its queued prompts are cancelled, the device gets `session.stop`, `stopped_by_agent` is true, and `work_session.archived` records `byAgentId`.
- **Only its own sessions (E3).** Atlas prompting or stopping any of these answers `not_your_session`, and nothing changes:
  - a person's session
  - Echo's session
  - an organization-B session (id guessed)

  The refusal is a denied `agent_tool_calls` row and does not count against the turn.
- **People keep control (principle 4).**
  - Ana is in the audience. She prompts the bot's session over HTTP; her prompt queues after Atlas's and runs as her own turn, one executor at a time.
  - An admin archives the session, and `stopped_by_agent` stays false.
  - Bob reads `#eng` but is outside the session's audience, so he gets 404.
- **Refusals.**
  - Prompting an archived session answers `session_ended`, and so does stopping it.
  - With the consent revoked, a prompt answers `profile_inactive` with `no_consent`, but the stop still works.
  - At 3 prompts per hour, the start and two prompts are queued and the fourth answers `prompt_rate` (E2).
  - `/model opus` answers `prompt_not_allowed`.
  - On a tainted turn, the prompt answers `outside_content`, but the stop still works.
  - After a session read, prompting and stopping are still allowed (D-20).
- **Audit, tenant isolation, leak sweep.**
  - Each of 12 outcomes (queued, stopped and the refusals) leaves exactly one `bot.work_session_*` row, with no metadata key beyond the allowed ids.
  - Organization B's token answers 404 `unknown_turn` on both routes. The call is recorded by `auditUnknownTurn` in B, and nothing is claimed.
  - `OCW-MARKER-0716-prompt` appears only in `work_prompts.body` and the session detail. It is in no audit row, tool call, bot row, card or log line.
