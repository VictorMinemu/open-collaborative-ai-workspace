## Phase Goal

See `07.16-01-PLAN.md` (PD-07, PD-08, PD-13, PD-23, Cross-plan contracts › Dispatch). **This plan's slice:** the bot's caps
re-checked on every turn by the one arbiter and by re-authorization, and the two route bodies the bot routes must share
with people (prompt, archive) extracted so nothing is copied.

<objective>
Make `tryDispatch` load the bot's layer for agent-authored prompts (refusing `bot_not_allowed` when the profile is
inactive), make `reauthorize` agent-aware for bot sessions (lower settings, interrupt an agent turn its layer no longer
allows), and extract `submitWorkPrompt`/`archiveWorkSession` into `controller.ts` used by the existing routes (D-02,
D-04, D-07, D-08, PD-07, PD-08, PD-13).

Purpose: RESEARCH risk 2: `tryDispatch` is the hottest Work Zone path; the agent branch is taken only for agent-authored
prompts and bot sessions, and every human suite stays green.
Output: service changes, a new controller module, the routes calling it, one integration file.
</objective>
