## Phase Goal

See `07.14-01-PLAN.md`. **This plan's slice (tracer, OAuth-client tier):** the 07.4 OAuth client learns client
credentials (discovery without the code-flow requirements, the grant, re-grant inputs) and names the failed step of any
sign-in, plus the dry-run diagnosis the Test sign-in button runs. No routes, no storage (07 and 09 own them).

<objective>
In `connectors/oauth.ts`: a `step` on `ConnectorOAuthError` set at every throw site, `discoverTokenEndpoint`,
`clientCredentialsGrant`, `previewClient` (the registration-free client decision, used by `chooseClient`). In the new
`connectors/diagnosis.ts`: `authFailureOf` and `diagnose`. All proven against the fake through the egress guard.

Purpose: 07 records `auth_failure` from `step`; 09 stores client-credential bundles and serves Test sign-in with these.
Output: the extended OAuth client, the diagnosis module, their unit and integration tests.
</objective>
