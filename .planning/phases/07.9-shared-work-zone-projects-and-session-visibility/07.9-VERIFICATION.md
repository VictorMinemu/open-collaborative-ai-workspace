  (the `ws:` authorizer, 96-142), `service.ts` (delivery recipients, 360-530), `org-queue.ts`, `routes.ts` (admin-view
  audit, 379-405), `apps/api/src/modules/realtime/service.ts` (invalidate-before-publish, 204-250).
- **W2 web list after a role downgrade:** `SESSION_ACCESS_EVENTS` (`apps/web/lib/workzone-queries.ts:257-262`) has no
  `member.updated`. An ex-admin's live view is revoked (`cause: role`), but their list keeps rows until the next refetch.
- **W3 queue latency (ADR-0030 deferred #330, #331):** `work_session.removed` and deliveries share one lane per
  organization; during a large sweep, list-row removal can trail by tens of seconds. The queue is in memory, and a
  frame from another transaction can land in the window between a commit and its invalidation (one Redis round trip).
  Live views are revoked at commit, so SC3 holds for live views; for list rows it holds outside heavy sweeps.
- **W4 ledger hygiene:** #313 (`e2e/tsconfig.json` exists, `8569c1cb`), #315 (`not_in_project` fixed, `4b923dc4`) and
  #324 (buttons fixed, only the faint dark disabled Switch remains) are still `open`.
