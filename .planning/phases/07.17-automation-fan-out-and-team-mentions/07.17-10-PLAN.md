## Phase Goal

See `07.17-01-PLAN.md` (PD-09, PD-15). **This plan's slice:** the cell turns each opened item into its own turn with the
item as outside content, tells run turns how to fan out, and tells both whom to mention.

<objective>
Map `agent.automation_item.v1` to a tainted turn in the opened root's thread with the item in its own untrusted block,
subscribe it in the bridge, and add the fan-out and escalation sentences to the automation block of run and item turns
(D-02, D-04, D-08, D-10, PD-09, PD-15).

Purpose: the item turn is the per-order worker of the owner's scenario; the sentences make the model call the tool once
and mention the right people.
Output: mapper and bridge changes with tests.
</objective>
