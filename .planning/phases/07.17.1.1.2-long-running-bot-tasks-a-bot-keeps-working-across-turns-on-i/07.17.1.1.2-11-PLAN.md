## Phase Goal

See `07.17.1.1.2-01-PLAN.md` (PD-20, PD-24). **This plan's slice (the browser end to end):** the owner's scenario in
the browser: a bot keeps working on a request over several turns by itself, visible on one live card, bounded, and
stoppable.

<objective>
Teach the fake cell the three task tools (directives, a `taskCallsFor` hook for `agent.task.v1`, done reporting, quiet
turns) with unit tests, and write `e2e/tests/bot-tasks.spec.ts`: Settings sets 4 turns per task; a task runs four
turns by itself and stops at the budget with one mention; a second task is stopped from the card; desktop and mobile,
axe in light and dark (D-02, D-04, D-05, D-11, D-14).

Purpose: the one browser proof that the whole chain (API scheduling, card, Stop, settings) works together.
Output: fake cell changes with tests, one helper, one spec.
</objective>
