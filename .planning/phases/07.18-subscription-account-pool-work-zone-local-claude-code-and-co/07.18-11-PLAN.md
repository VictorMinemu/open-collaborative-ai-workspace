## Phase Goal

See 07.18-01 (the hub). **This plan's slice:** Codex runs each Work Zone session under the `CODEX_HOME` of the slot the
server chose, moves to another slot between turns by restarting its app-server and resuming the same thread, and
reports usage-limit errors and rate-limit readings.
