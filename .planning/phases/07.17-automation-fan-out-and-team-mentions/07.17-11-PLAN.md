## Phase Goal

See `07.17-01-PLAN.md` (PD-07, PD-08, PD-15). **This plan's slice:** a fan-out run turn is offered `ocw_conversation_open`
and told its caps and whom to mention; nothing else changes for any other turn.

<objective>
Add the caller-computed `conversationOpen` parameter to `turnPolicy`, carry it on `AgentMention` from
`deliverAutomationRun`, compute `fanOutOffer` and the escalation in the runner, and put `fanOut` and `escalation` on the
run event; prove the offer and the byte-identical turns elsewhere (D-02, D-08, PD-07, PD-08, PD-15).

Purpose: 16's route requires the tool on the turn; 10's plugin renders the fields.
Output: four source files and one integration file.
</objective>
