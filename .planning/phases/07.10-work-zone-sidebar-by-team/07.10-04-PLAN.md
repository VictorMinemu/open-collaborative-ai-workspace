## Phase Goal

See `07.10-01-PLAN.md`. **This plan's slice:** the sidebar tells each person what waits on them ("Needs you"), keeps
history one click away (Settled), finds anything (grouped search) and works from the keyboard; today's flat search and
Settled block are replaced, with nothing the viewer had lost.

<objective>
Add Needs you (D-05) from 02's `attention` and a per-browser seen map (R10, PD-09), Settled as a collapsed section in
the viewer's scope (D-04), grouped search (D-08, R9), the sessions-request states, the 44 px header "+" and search field,
and arrow-key navigation with focus kept on live updates (C-02). Retire the old three-line active row once nothing uses
it, moving its tests. Update the Settled and search parts of the existing specs (R7).

Purpose: WZS-01, WZS-02, WZS-04, WZS-05, WZS-08, WZS-09, WZS-11.
Output: the attention, Settled, search and keys pieces under `components/work-zone/sidebar/`, the seen-map writer in
the session view's data hook, `work-zone-sidebar-attention.spec.ts`, two specs updated, 4 screenshot names.
</objective>
