## Phase Goal

Each member connects their own MCP, ERP or CRM account once, and secrets are used by reference (plan map and executor
rules: 07.4-01-PLAN.md).

**This plan's slice (tracer, member UI):** a member opens Settings › Connections, clicks Connect, signs in at the fake
provider in a real browser and comes back Connected; then every Connections state, the return banner with Back to
chat, shares and the desktop hand-off; then Keychain, the admin's private network hosts and the per-member Re-probe on
the MCP page. The visual and copy contract is `07.4-UI-SPEC.md` (§§ 1-3, Copywriting, Accessibility, Screenshots);
where this plan and the spec differ, the spec wins. The bot-side journey (connect card → connect → the bot answers) is
07.4-14.

against the fake provider.
