# Phase 07.9 gap: per-organization background fan-out and privacy-first live re-decisions Summary

Requests that change Work Zone access now return right after their commit, and the removed viewers lose their live view
before anything else about the session reaches them. The per-user session delivery and the re-authorization sweeps now
run in an ordered, per-organization queue in the API process. Revoking a subscription does not wait for that queue: every
gateway holds the affected subscriptions from the moment the access change is published. The ledger item is fixed too: a
deactivated member's re-decision now has cause `role`, not `visibility`.

### Invariant: privacy first (tests)

- **Same transaction.** `appendEvent` now collects invalidation scopes per root transaction (`TxContext.root`, shared by
  savepoints). Every frame hook of that transaction publishes them before its own frame. Each process has one Redis
  publisher and one subscriber connection, so every gateway receives the invalidation before any frame of that commit.
  - Test: `realtime-reauth.int` "one transaction". The frame is appended before the event that removes access, and only
    `revoked` arrives. It was RED on the old code (the `ev` arrived).
- **Later frames.** When an invalidation matches a live subscription, the gateway holds it. Frames wait, at most 1,000,
  and then the client gets `resync`. A denial drops the held frames with the subscription. An allow delivers them,
  durable frames in seq order. Replay and buffered frames during a subscribe follow the same hold.
  - Test: `realtime-reauth.int` "held". It was RED on the old code.
- **Work Zone.** Ana narrows a session to `owner`, then the device sends a durable event and a delta. Pia receives no
  `ev`/`eph` frame for that session, only `revoked` (cause `visibility`). On her `user:` topic she gets exactly one
  `work_session.removed` and no `work_session.updated`. Ana receives all of it.
  - Test: `work-session-sentinel.int`. It was RED on the old realtime code: the narrowing's own `visibility_changed` and
    snapshot frames reached Pia.
- **Async path.** The test holds the organization's queue with a gate task, queues a delivery, then narrows.
  - The PUT returns while everything waits.
  - Pia's `ws:` view is revoked while the queue is still held, so revocation does not depend on the queue.
  - Nothing reaches `user:` until the gate opens.
  - Then the delivery that was queued before the narrowing runs after it. It reads the narrowed audience, so it skips Pia.
  - The `removed` notice follows it.
  - Test: `work-session-sentinel.int`, "the request returns while the background queue waits".

Ordering per session: one organization's tasks run one at a time, in the order they were queued. A delivery coalesces
only into the delivery waiting at the tail of the queue. `work_session.removed` now goes through the queue (before this
change it was published directly after commit), so an update for a person is never delivered after their `removed`.

### Ledger: the deactivated cause

The `ws:` authorizer runs the backstop hide path. If the session exists but the member is deactivated or missing, the
cause is now `role`. Test: `realtime-reauth.int`, "a deactivated member's live view is re-decided as `role`". It was RED
(`visibility`) on the old code.

## Shutdown (documented in `OrgQueue.close`)

The work-session module's `close` (run by `app.close()` before the database closes) drains the queue for up to 5 s. It
then drops what still waits, logs the kinds and the count, and refuses new work. Nothing dropped widens access:

- Live subscriptions were already re-decided by the invalidation published at commit. The gateway's periodic re-check
  and every new subscribe decide again.
- A dropped delivery is covered by the list's refetch on reconnect.
- A dropped sweep leaves the stored settings above a narrower policy until the next sweep. Every turn is still capped by
  its author's current access when it is dispatched (P-07). The only leftover is that a Full access turn already in
  flight is not interrupted.

## Multi-instance

The behaviour is unchanged. Each instance queues the work of its own commits and re-decides its own sockets'
subscriptions from the shared invalidation channel. The hold is per instance, so the "held" and "one transaction" tests
run on a second gateway with its own realtime service on the stack's database and Redis.

## Deviations

1. **[Rule 2] Gateway hold and invalidations-first ordering.** These were not in the brief's "Do" list, but the
   invariant needs them: before this change, the narrowing's own snapshot and any later frame reached the removed
   viewer's `ws:` subscription until the re-decision ran (RED test above). Queuing deliveries alone could not fix that.
2. **[Rule 1] `work_session.removed` goes through the queue.** Published directly after commit, it could arrive before a
   delivery that was queued earlier, and the client would put the row back.
3. **[Rule 1] `onAccessChanged` invalidates before it sweeps.** It used to invalidate after the sweep, which delayed the
   revocation by the sweep's duration (about 7 s at 250 sessions).
4. **No re-read of in-flight deliveries.** I considered re-reading a delivery when an invalidation arrives mid-read. I
   dropped it: it could not be tested deterministically, and queue ordering already guarantees convergence (candidate 2).
5. **Tests only through `pnpm verify`.** Plans 13 and 14 files were not touched. Neither were STATE.md, ROADMAP.md or
   WINDOWS.md.

## Broken-window candidates (for the coordinator; the ledger was not touched)

1. **#311: candidate to close.** The request no longer waits for the fan-out or the sweeps (numbers above).
2. **(low) In-flight prior delivery.** A delivery whose read began before a narrowing committed can publish just after
   it, with the pre-narrowing DTO the person could see at read time. It always lands before the `removed` notice, so the
   client converges. This replaces 07.9-07's candidate 1; the window is now one read.
3. **(low) Cross-transaction window.** A frame from a different transaction, published between a narrowing's COMMIT and
   the moment its invalidation reaches Redis (one round trip), is not held. Same-transaction frames and everything after
   the invalidation are held.
4. **(medium-low) One lane per organization.** A 2,000-session sweep holds that organization's lane for about 12.7 s, and
   its deliveries for another 37 s. Other deliveries of that organization wait behind them; other organizations do not.
   Follow-up: separate lanes for sweeps and deliveries, or batch `sessionFactsForUsers` across sessions (about 18 ms per
   session at 200 members).
5. **(low) In-memory queue.** A crash loses waiting sweeps. Settings can stay above a narrowed policy until the next
   sweep; every turn is still capped (P-07). Follow-up: a sweep at startup or a durable outbox.

## Decisions for ADR-0030

- **Invalidate before publish.** All access invalidations of a transaction are published before any of its frames.
- **Hold while re-deciding.**
  - A matched subscription buffers its frames until the re-decision answers.
  - Denied: the frames are dropped. Allowed: they are delivered in order.
  - Authorizer error: the subscription is kept, the frames are dropped and the client gets `resync`.
  - The limit is 1,000 held frames, then `resync`.
- **After-commit work goes to an in-process, per-organization queue.**
  - What is queued: per-user deliveries, `removed` notices, own-narrowing snapshots, policy and device sweeps.
  - Ordering: one lane per organization, at most 2 organizations at once.
  - Coalescing: a delivery only into the delivery waiting at the tail; a sweep is dropped when the same sweep still waits.
  - Retries: 3 runs in place (100 ms, then 500 ms), logged with ids and an error code only.
  - Shutdown: drain for up to 5 s, then abandon.
- **Sweeps run as the system.** Each runs in 50-session transactions and reads the policy again for each one. The policy
  sweep keeps the admin as the audit actor. Device changes invalidate first, then queue the sweep.
- **Revocation never waits for the queue.** The live view is revoked at commit; deliveries trail. Re-authorization of a
  session's stored settings is eventual, with the per-turn cap (P-07) as the enforcement point meanwhile.
- **A deactivated member's re-decision has cause `role`.**
