## Phase Goal

See `07.14-01-PLAN.md`. **This plan's slice (tracer, registry tier):** the MCP server registry admits the two
organization modes with their field rules, protects the organization's stored credential with a confirmation on a mode
switch or a delete, keeps OAuth client registrations across the two sign-in modes, and shows the organization connection
and the last failure in its DTO — while organization-mode servers never reach the cell.

<objective>
Change `apps/api/src/modules/mcp/service.ts` (create, update, delete, DTO, probe refusal, cell rendering) for the
organization modes per PD-03, PD-05, PD-11; export `setAuthFailure` from `mcp/index.ts`; extend `mcp.int.test.ts`.

Purpose: the dialog (12) creates these servers, the row (11) shows `orgConnection`, and 07/09 record failures through
`setAuthFailure`.
Output: the registry changes and their integration tests.
</objective>
