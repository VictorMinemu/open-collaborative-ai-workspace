# Phase 07.16 Plan 15: A bot starts its own Work Zone session, as its sponsor Summary

**A member asks a team bot in a channel and the bot starts the Work Zone session itself. The session runs in the sponsor's name on the bot's server, within the profile's caps, seen by the people the server picks, with a live card in the asker's thread. Nobody presses Start. 25/25 integration cases pass locally on real PostgreSQL and Redis.**

## Intended change (CLAUDE.md plan mode: permissions, audit, tenant data)

- New cell route `POST /agent-channel/v1/work-sessions/start`. tx0 has no user: it finds the turn of this bot and its profile. tx1 runs as the sponsor and checks, in order: the live turn, the tool on the turn, the profile status, session output and taint, then the claim. After that, `WorkSessionModule.bots.start` runs the caps, the audience, `createWorkSession({ agent })`, the `bot_work_sessions` row, the card and the audit.
- No permission check, RLS policy or approval gate is weakened. The new SQL function only counts rows, so the caps can see sessions the sponsor cannot. It returns no rows to the caller.
- The claim gains two opt-in options. Its one new condition (`session_output_read = false`) is true for every turn until 17 sets the flag, so existing callers behave as before. The `tool-calls` and `tool-approvals` suites pass unchanged.
- Human `POST /work-sessions` is untouched.

## Accomplishments

- **Phase API end to end (success criterion 1).** Admin Víctor saves Atlas's profile on a service FakeDevice that has the isolated-server mark. Ana mentions Atlas in `#eng`. The start answers `started` with `mode: fullAccess`, `model: opus` and `projectName: billing-api`. In the session row, `created_by` is Víctor, `started_by_agent_id` is Atlas and the mode is `fullAccess`. The prompt's author is Víctor and its agent is Atlas. The device receives `session.start` and then the prompt with `permissionMode: fullAccess`.
- **Live card.** It is platform-owned, of kind `work_zone`, and sits in Ana's thread. The title is "Work Zone · billing-api". The fields follow `WORK_SESSION_CARD_LABELS` order, with Step "Starting". Its actions are Open session (`/work-zone/<id>`) and Follow. The card never shows the prompt, the title or a team project name.
- **Caps.** Two locks are taken in order (`bot_ws:<agent>`, then `bot_ws_org:<org>`), and the counts come from a definer function. The refusals are `too_many_sessions` (E1 is exact under two parallel starts), `org_limit`, `prompt_rate` (the start's own prompt counts), `project_not_allowed` and `tree_busy`.
- **Audience (PD-11), decided by the server.** A public channel gives `project`. A private channel gives `people` (members who can see the project, minus the sponsor). A DM gives `people` [asker] or `owner`. A body with `visibility`, `deviceId`, `userId` or `worktree` answers 400.
- **Claim options (D-08, D-20).** `outsideContent` and `sessionOutputExempt`. After a session read, every other side-effecting claim is refused as `tainted`, unless the call is exempt or a person granted it. A start after a read answers `session_output`, and a start on a tainted turn answers `outside_content`. Both are recorded as denied calls that do not count against the turn.
- **Tenant and identity.**
  - Organization B's cell token with organization A's event answers 404 `unknown_turn`.
  - So do another bot's event and an asker who no longer reads the conversation.
  - A deleted profile answers `profile_inactive`; a disabled bot answers `profile_inactive` with reason `bot_disabled`.
  - The leak sweep: the marker appears in no audit row, `agent_tool_calls` row, card or bot row, and in no API log line (LOG_LEVEL debug).

## Deviations from Plan

### Auto-fixed Issues

**1. [Rule 3 - Blocking] Caps could not be counted in the sponsor's transaction**
- **Found during:** Task 2
- **Issue:** the `audience_backstop` on `work_sessions` and `work_prompts` hides every session the sponsor has no relation to. `org_limit` and `tree_busy` (another person's session) would undercount, and so would the bot's own count after a sponsor change.
- **Fix:** a narrow `SECURITY DEFINER` function `app.bot_work_session_load(agent, project, states)` in `post-migrate.sql`, following the `work_project_repo_session_count` precedent. It returns counts and one flag only. A separate user-less connection was rejected because it can deadlock the pool under the locks, and switching identity mid-transaction was rejected as an RLS workaround.
- **Files modified:** packages/db/sql/post-migrate.sql, apps/api/src/modules/work-session/bot-sessions.ts
- **Commit:** 350050d1

**2. [Rule 2 - Missing critical] `/memories` has its own taint UPDATE**
- **Found during:** Task 3
- **Issue:** the plan's D-20 case names `/memories`, but `saveMemory` does not go through `claimToolCall`. A cell calling `/memories` directly would skip the session-output rule.
- **Fix:** the same condition and re-read in `saveMemory` (one line each). The D-20 test drives the gate claim directly (`claimToolCall` for `ocw_memory_save`). It does not go through `/memories` over HTTP, because a team bot's DM turn does not reliably carry the memory tool in this fixture.
- **Files modified:** apps/api/src/modules/bot-capabilities/memory.ts
- **Commit:** 63cdabd2

**3. [Plan detail] A-01's second-transaction card post was not built**
- The card post as the bot, in the sponsor's transaction, passed every case. If chat ever refuses it, the start's transaction rolls back (no session without its card). There is no silent second path.

**4. [Plan detail] `botSessionAudience(tx, p)` takes no `perms`**
- P comes from the SQL rule and `checkAudience`, and R's readers come in as `isReadable`. The grep only checks the export name.

**5. [Plan detail] Settings and mode checks are not pre-checked in bot-sessions**
- The prohibition "No check of createWorkSession is copied" wins. `createWorkSession` with the agent layer refuses them, and its failures are mapped. As a result, `tree_busy` is decided before the settings, not after as in PD-10's list. The outcome is the same, because every refusal comes before any write.

**Total deviations:** 2 auto-fixed (1 blocking, 1 missing critical), 3 plan details. **Impact:** no scope change. Every must-have is covered by a passing integration case.
