## Phase Goal

A shared agent acts with the credentials of the person who asked — each member connects their own account once,
sessions refresh themselves, and secrets are used by reference and never seen by the model — and people start their
own agent from a template (plan map and executor rules: 07.4-01-PLAN.md).

**This plan's slice (tracer, the user journey):** the connect card in a DM leads a member through Settings to the
provider and back, and the same bot then answers with that member's account; then the shared-conversation and
keychain journeys and the full gate; then the ADR, the operator page, CODEOWNERS and AGENTS.md.
