## Objective

Per D-21 … D-27, D-32, PD-15 … PD-17 (hub `07.13-01-PLAN.md`). The device half of sign-ins: the agent asks for one, the
member's hand back exports and uploads only that site's state, a later turn of the same member loads it into a fresh
site-locked in-memory page, and anyone else's turn (or the end of the member's own turn) re-seals and unloads it.

Purpose: WZB-09 and WZB-10 on the device. Output: sign-in module, the eighth tool, export/lock/load ops, manager
gates, tests against the fixture login site with a fake API.
