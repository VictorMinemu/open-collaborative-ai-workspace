## Phase Goal

**As a** member of a team that works in the workspace, **I want to** hold my whole daily conversation there — channels grouped by department and team, DMs and group DMs with people and bots, messages I can edit, delete, react to, pin, save and link to, @channel/@here and notifications I control, search and files — **so that** I do not need a second chat tool and every message stays attributed, editable under audit and delivered in realtime.

**This plan's slice (tracer):** Ada @mentions Sam; a notification row is written in the message transaction, `notification.created` reaches Sam's `user:` stream, his Mentions view and count update live, and opening it jumps to the message. Then DMs, thread replies, @channel/@here, edits and privacy. Planner decisions P-01 … P-12 are in `04-01-PLAN.md` (P-03, P-04, P-05, P-08, P-09, P-11).

<objective>
Create the Notification module: recipients computed in the message transaction (mentions, DMs, group DMs, thread replies, @channel, @here), durable `user:` events, the inbox as the Mentions view, @channel/@here with an org online set, permission and rate limit, and correct behaviour on edits and lost access.

Purpose: CHAT-08 (badges and read state are Plan 10; preferences and OS notifications are Plan 11).
Output: `lib/chat-events.ts`, `modules/notification/*`, `realtime/online.ts`, Mentions view, composer @channel/@here, reserved mention chips, `notifications.int.test.ts`, `e2e/tests/notifications.spec.ts`.
</objective>
