# Phase 07.17.1.1.2 Plan 04: "Allow long tasks" policy, limits and per-bot toggle Summary

**`longTasks` capability (bot toggle on by default, organization switch with personal sub-switch) and `BotPolicy.tasks` (40 turns / 8 h / 10 running, caps 200 / 24 / 100) stored in `bot_policies`, decided by `loadTaskPolicy` and exposed per turn as `computeTurn.longTasks`, with a Long tasks row and limits section in Settings › Bot policy and a Long tasks toggle on each bot's capabilities**

## Intended change (CLAUDE.md plan mode: policy, permissions, audit)

- No new permission: `PUT /api/v1/bot-policy` keeps `bot.manage`, `PATCH /agents/:id/capabilities` keeps `bot.read` (404) then `bot.configure` (403).
- Effective long tasks = bot toggle ∧ organization `orgWide` ∧ (personal bot ? `personal` : true); a bot toggle can never exceed the organization's switch.
- Limits bounded in the contract (400) and by the 03 database checks; every changed limit audited as `tasks.<key>` in `bot_policy.updated` (keys only), every toggle as `longTasks` in `agent.capabilities_updated`.
- The policy row is tenant-scoped (RLS); organization B reads its own defaults.
- No tool is offered by this plan; 07 gates the task tools on `computeTurn.longTasks`.
