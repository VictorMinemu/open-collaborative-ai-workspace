## Phase Goal

See `07.16-01-PLAN.md` (PD-16, PD-12, E6–E8). **This plan's slice:** the bot orchestrates without polling: every
meaningful transition of its session wakes it in the thread it started from, bounded and idempotent, and the live card
follows the session.

Purpose: success criterion 2 and BWZ-03; ADR-0028's ids-only jobs; the automation anchor's delivery pattern.

## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| device events → session state → job | Transitions come from the device; the job carries ids only |
| server → cell | A server-originated bot turn (the second allowed trigger, ADR-0038) |

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-07.16-20-01 | Denial of service | wake loops (wake → prompt → turn → wake) | medium | mitigate | Row-flag debounce, idempotent `last_woken_*`, daily wake budget, prompts/hour, the bot's daily turn limit; tests E6–E8 |
| T-07.16-20-02 | Elevation of privilege | confused deputy: posting where the requester cannot read | high | mitigate | Requester read and active check before delivery; chat's reply check applies to the bot's post |
| T-07.16-20-03 | Spoofing | forged job ids across tenants | high | mitigate | Ids-only payload re-read under `withTenant`; organization-B case |
| T-07.16-20-04 | Information disclosure | content in summary, jobs, audit, logs | high | mitigate | Summary from structured facts only; ids in jobs and audits; leak sweep |
| T-07.16-20-05 | Information disclosure | card per-viewer data | medium | mitigate | `composeWorkSessionCard` fields only (viewer-neutral) |
| T-07.16-20-06 | Tampering | taint lost across a wake | medium | mitigate | `taintedAtStart` from the session's sticky `tainted_at` |
</threat_model>
