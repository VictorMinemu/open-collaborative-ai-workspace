## Phase Goal

See `07.9-01-PLAN.md`. **This plan's slice:** team projects become manageable Control Plane entities — created under a
team or department by the people ADR-0024 lets act there, with members, assigned teams, linked device repositories and
one default — and their access is a resolver decision on a new `work_project` resource.

`work_project.updated` org event, and `Project.teamProject` with the D-13 filter on `GET /projects`. Every change is
audited (C-02).

</objective>

## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| browser → /work-projects | Ids of units, teams, repos and devices are untrusted until resolved inside `withTenant` |
| project management → session visibility | Membership, team and repo changes widen or narrow who sees sessions |

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-07.9-04-01 | Elevation of privilege | a lead acting on a unit outside their subtree | high | mitigate | `work_project.manage` decided on the parent / project resource with its path (ADR-0024 lead grants); tests per role (Tasks 1–2) |
| T-07.9-04-02 | Elevation of privilege | linking someone else's device repo to gain visibility | high | mitigate | `device.use` required to link and unlink (Task 2) |
| T-07.9-04-03 | Information disclosure | project existence leak | medium | mitigate | `hideAs404` with identical bodies for forbidden and unknown ids; index lists only openable projects (Tasks 1, 3) |
| T-07.9-04-04 | Tampering | cross-tenant ids in team/repo requests | high | mitigate | Every id loaded inside `withTenant`; composite FKs; org-B cases (Task 2) |
| T-07.9-04-05 | Repudiation | silent structure changes | medium | mitigate | Audit per change (ids only); `work_project.updated` event; unit delete refused while repos are linked (Task 2) |
| T-07.9-04-06 | Information disclosure | names on the org stream | medium | mitigate | `work_project.updated` carries `{ unitId }` only (Task 2 test) |
