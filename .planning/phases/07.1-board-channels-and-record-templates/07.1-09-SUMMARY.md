## Deviations from Plan

1. **[Rule 3 – blocking] The realtime client follows the browser's network** (`b48f4ed`, `packages/realtime-client/src/client.ts`, `apps/web/lib/realtime.tsx`).
   - **Problem:** under `context.setOffline(true)`, Chromium keeps the open WebSocket alive. `useConnectionMeta().status` stayed `open` and the banner could never appear. A real dead network behaves the same way for minutes: the client pings every 25 s but never times out a missing pong.
   - **Fix:** `RealtimeClient.setNetwork(online)`.
     - Offline drops the socket and detaches its handlers. Status becomes `closed` and nothing is retried.
     - Online reconnects at once instead of waiting out a backoff of up to 30 s. Every topic re-subscribes from its cursor, so the server replays what was missed.
     - A client the app closed stays closed.
   - **Wiring:** `RealtimeProvider` connects it to `window` `online` / `offline`.
   - **Not in `files_modified`:** none of the wave-4 plans own these files.
2. **[UI-SPEC, copy choice] Offline copy while the client is `reconnecting`, if `navigator.onLine` is false.** The UI-SPEC maps `reconnecting` to the reconnecting copy. When the browser itself has no network, "You're offline…" is the accurate copy. The reconnecting copy still shows for a socket that drops while the network is up; this is unit-tested but not screenshotted.
3. **[UI-SPEC, visual] The banner is `min-h-8`, not a fixed 32 px.** At 390 the fixed copy does not fit on one line, so it wraps rather than truncating.
4. **[P-12 implementation] The board stays mounted under Messages, hidden with `display:none`.** The plan asks that switching views keep the 390 tab and the scroll. The tab already lived in the URL (`col`), but the scroll died with the unmount. A probe confirmed that Chromium keeps `scrollTop` / `scrollLeft` across `display:none`. The Messages view itself is unchanged.
5. **[Scope] Board-layout permalink fallback.** A top-level link that is not a cached card switches to `view=messages` and keeps `m`, so text-channel permalink logic shows and highlights it. That covers a plain message on a board, and a card beyond the first 100 of its column. Covered by e2e for a plain message.
6. **[Scope] Extra regression spec:** `presence.spec`, because the realtime client changed. It passed in both projects.
