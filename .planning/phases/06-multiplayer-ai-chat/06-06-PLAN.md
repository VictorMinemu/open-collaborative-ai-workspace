## Phase Goal

**As a** member who holds a private AI chat, **I want to** share it in place with a colleague, a team, a department or the whole organization so that several of us and the model write in the same conversation — the model knowing who said what and answering one request at a time on the asker's own plan or model, nobody's private context leaking, and agents joining as full personas that post live cards — **so that** AI work in the workspace becomes multiplayer, attributable and safe.

**This plan's slice:** the single execution authority for a shared chat's model (principle 4): addressing, a message-derived queue, coalesced follow-ups, requester-pays and crash pickup, with the UI arriving in 06-11. P-decisions: `06-01-PLAN.md` (P-07, P-08).

<objective>
Make shared AI chats take messages from several people at once while exactly one generation runs: addressed messages start or queue, the finalize hook starts the next coalesced turn for the first waiting author, skips are explicit, and the queue is visible to everyone.

Purpose: MULTI-04 (D-01, D-05; P-07, P-08).
Output: `queue.ts`, multiplayer branch of the send route (201 started / 202 posted), regenerate/queue rule, sweeper pickup, `app.pending_ai_queues`, `queue.int.test.ts`.
</objective>

## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| participants → send route | Many controllers, one execution authority (principle 4) |
| finalize transaction → next generation | Starts work on another person's backend: must be that person's own |
| sweeper → all organizations | SECURITY DEFINER ids-only lookup, then per-org tenant transactions |

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-06-06-01 | Elevation of privilege | coalesced turn billed to the owner or to someone who did not ask | high | mitigate | Requester = first waiting author, resolved with that author's own model ref; usage requester asserted (Task 1) |
| T-06-06-02 | Denial of service | queue flooding / runaway metered turns | medium | mitigate | `AI_QUEUE_MAX` (429), per-user concurrency and daily caps checked per started turn, coalescing |
| T-06-06-03 | Tampering | concurrent generations on one conversation | medium | mitigate | Stream lock in `startNext`, partial unique index backstop asserted (Task 2) |
| T-06-06-04 | Elevation of privilege | participants stopping or regenerating others' answers | medium | mitigate | Requester-or-owner stop; regenerate blocked while a queue exists (Task 2) |
| T-06-06-05 | Information disclosure | cross-tenant sweeper access | medium | mitigate | `pending_ai_queues` returns ids only, PUBLIC revoked; work happens in each org's `withTenant` (Task 2) |
| T-06-06-06 | Repudiation | who triggered a queued turn | low | mitigate | Audit `ai_generation.queued_started` with message ids and requester (ids only) |
</threat_model>
