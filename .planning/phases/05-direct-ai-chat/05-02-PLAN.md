## Phase Goal

**As a** member of an organization that uses the workspace, **I want to** hold a private, streaming AI conversation on my own ChatGPT plan by default or on a model my organization pays for — choosing the model per message, stopping and regenerating answers, with titles, instructions, web search, files, a usage record for every answer and a read-only share link for colleagues — **so that** I get a ChatGPT/Claude.ai-quality assistant inside the workspace where every generation is metered, private by default and attributable.

**This plan's slice (tracer):** an admin's Anthropic key, stored as a SecretReference, reaches a member's AI chat: the member picks "Claude Sonnet 5.5 · METERED" in the new model picker and the answer streams from the fake Anthropic endpoint through the 05-01 run loop. Then the cell moves to SecretReferences, the allowed list is proven to govern chats and bots, and per-message switching is proven. Planner decisions P-01 … P-14 are in `05-01-PLAN.md` (P-04 gateway, P-08 executor rules, P-09 UI, P-10 secrets, P-11 labels).
