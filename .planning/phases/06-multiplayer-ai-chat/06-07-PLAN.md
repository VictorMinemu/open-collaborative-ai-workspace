## Phase Goal

**As a** member who holds a private AI chat, **I want to** share it in place with a colleague, a team, a department or the whole organization so that several of us and the model write in the same conversation — the model knowing who said what and answering one request at a time on the asker's own plan or model, nobody's private context leaking, and agents joining as full personas that post live cards — **so that** AI work in the workspace becomes multiplayer, attributable and safe.

**This plan's slice:** agents become full personas — the Phase 1 one-click bots gain a scope (organization, department, team, project, personal), an owner and Team Lead creation, with tools configured per persona (Phase 3.1 toggles and grants). P-decisions: `06-01-PLAN.md` (P-04).

<objective>
Extend bots into scoped personas: `agents.unit_id` drives scope, visibility and lead rights through the resolver; admins and unit leads create and configure them (tools included) in the Bots UI.

Purpose: AGENT-01 (D-07; P-04).
Output: agent contract additions, create/update with unit and owner, unit visibility precondition, lead rights, unit deletion guard, Bots UI scope/owner fields, `personas.int.test.ts`, `agent-personas.spec.ts`.
</objective>

| member client → bot lists, mentions | Visibility decided per persona by the resolver |

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-06-07-01 | Elevation of privilege | a lead creating or configuring personas (and their tools) outside their units | high | mitigate | `bot.manage_unit` / `bot.configure` only through `LEAD_PERMISSIONS` on the lead's subtree; resolver and int tests for Frontend/organization (Task 1) |
| T-06-07-02 | Information disclosure | unit persona listed or mentionable by non-members | medium | mitigate | `unit_agent` precondition on `bot.read`; list filter; mention dispatch requires `bot.read` (Task 1) |
| T-06-07-03 | Elevation of privilege | unit deletion silently widening a persona to the organization | medium | mitigate | FK RESTRICT + 409 `unit_has_bots` (Task 1) |
| T-06-07-04 | Tampering | owner set to a foreign or inactive user | low | mitigate | Owner must be an active member under tenant RLS (404) |
| T-06-07-05 | Repudiation | scope/owner changes | low | mitigate | `agent.created/updated` audits carry unit and owner ids |
</threat_model>
