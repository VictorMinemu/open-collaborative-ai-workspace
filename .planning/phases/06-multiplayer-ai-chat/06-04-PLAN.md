## Phase Goal

**As a** member who holds a private AI chat, **I want to** share it in place with a colleague, a team, a department or the whole organization so that several of us and the model write in the same conversation — the model knowing who said what and answering one request at a time on the asker's own plan or model, nobody's private context leaking, and agents joining as full personas that post live cards — **so that** AI work in the workspace becomes multiplayer, attributable and safe.

**This plan's slice:** from "shared with Alex" to "shared with Backend team / the Engineering department / everyone", with levels, level changes and live revocation — the PRD §17 path "Private AI Chat → Shared with Alex → Shared with Backend Team, without copy-pasting history". P-decisions: `06-01-PLAN.md`.

Output: grants.ts (PATCH, DELETE, unit/org, notices, invalidators), aiAccessSql unit/org, AI chat title search over the readable set, `grants.int.test.ts`, search and attachment cases.
</objective>

<threat_model>
## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| owner client → grant routes | Unit ids and levels are untrusted |
| unit/org hierarchy → access | Membership paths decide unit grants |
| live subscriptions | Must follow revocation without waiting for the 5-minute backstop |

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-06-04-01 | Information disclosure | revoked grantee keeps a live stream | high | mitigate | `ai_chat.access_changed` invalidator re-decides every `conv:` subscriber; `removeMember` emits `member_removed`; existing `units.updated` covers unit-membership changes; latency asserted (Task 1) |
| T-06-04-02 | Tampering | grant to another tenant's unit | high | mitigate | Unit must be an `org_units` row under tenant RLS (404); int test with an org-B unit |
| T-06-04-03 | Elevation of privilege | department grant reaching units outside its subtree, or stale participation rows granting access | high | mitigate | One `aiAccessSql` over membership paths; rows never decide access; matrix test for Jo/Riley/Robin/Sam |
| T-06-04-04 | Information disclosure | search or files of a shared chat visible to non-grantees or after revoke | high | mitigate | Title search over the readable set only; attachment reads via readable set; tests for Sam, admin, revoked Alex (Task 2) |
| T-06-04-05 | Repudiation | silent access changes | medium | mitigate | Notice rows visible to participants + audits `ai_chat.grant_created/updated/revoked` (ids only) |
| T-06-04-06 | Denial of service | notification floods on unit/org grants | low | mitigate | No per-member notification or participation rows for unit/org grants; one ids-only org event |
</threat_model>
