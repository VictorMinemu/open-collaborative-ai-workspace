## Phase Goal

**As an** Organization Admin and every member of the workspace, **I want to** arrange the company into departments, teams and projects with the four PRD roles, have every route decided by one permission resolver, and manage my own password, sessions and profile, **so that** each person and agent can do exactly what their role, units and rules allow and every sensitive change is attributed and audited.

**This plan's slice (AUTH-06 daemon connections):** the Device Link authenticates with short-lived device JWTs while installed desktop apps keep working behind a flag.

<objective>
Add the device-token → device-JWT exchange (research §Pattern 5 "Daemon"), verify device JWTs on `/runtime/ws` (research inventory M21) with a `device.link` resolver decision, keep legacy raw device tokens behind `OCW_ACCEPT_LEGACY_DEVICE_TOKEN` (D-03), and teach the daemon to fetch a token before each connect, falling back to the raw token only when the server has no exchange endpoint (older server).

Purpose: SC4 includes daemon connections; D-03 keeps the demo Mac working until Phase 8 replaces the device credential with Ed25519/RFC 8628.
Output: device/token.ts, JWT-aware link.ts, env flag, daemon exchange, tests.
</objective>
