# Phase 07.17 Plan 14: Fan-out and escalation in the automation editor, opened counts in run history Summary

**The automation editor now has "For each new item": a switch, then per-run and per-day caps (10 and 100 by default), shown only for agent-turn schedules into a channel or a board. It also has "When it needs people": a People / Teams picker with removable chips, up to five. Saves send `fanOut` and `escalation` only when those sections changed. The daily-limit callout adds the per-day cap, and each fan-out run in the history shows "Opened 2 conversations" or "Nothing new".**

## Accomplishments

- **For each new item** (`fan-out-section.tsx`). This sits after "Deliver to". It has a 44 px switch row, "Open a conversation for each new item", with a helper that names the target ("The bot opens a card in Backlog…" or "…a thread in #orders…"). When on, it shows "At most per run" and "At most per day" (one row of at most 28 rem on desktop, stacked at 390), the bounds and dedupe line, and "Runs are listed in one “Orders · Runs” thread." It is absent for webhook, mail, digest, DM and thread automations.
- **Save rules.** A new automation sends `fanOut` only when it is on. An existing one sends it only when the section changed. A fan-out automation moved to a target or trigger where fan-out is not allowed sends `enabled: false` with its stored caps. Errors show inline: "Use a whole number from 1 to 25 / 500" and "At most per day must be at least per run".
- **Daily-limit callout.** `expectedTurnsPerDay` gets `fanOutPerDayOf(allowed, fanOut)`, which is the per-day cap when fan-out is on and 0 otherwise.
- **When it needs people** (`escalation-picker.tsx`). This section is for agent-turn automations. Chips (team icon or avatar, name, "Remove <name>" at 44 px on phones) wrap. One combobox, "Add a person or team", lists People (avatar, name, `@handle`) and Teams (icon, name, `@handle`, "Team · 6" / "Department · 24"), never projects. Arrow keys, Enter and Escape work. At five chosen the field is disabled with the placeholder "Up to 5". The helper reads "They get a notification only if they can read <target>." A 400 `escalation_not_allowed` replaces the helper with "Someone here can't read <target> or left the workspace."
- **Error texts.** `fan_out_not_allowed`: "This automation can't open a conversation per item with this trigger or target."
- **Run history.** A neutral `run-opened` badge with `fanOutLabel(run.opened)` appears when `opened` is a number.

## Deviations from Plan

### Auto-fixed Issues

**1. [Rule 1 - UX correctness] Helper copy per target**
- **Found during:** Task 1
- **Issue:** PD-19's "a thread (or a card in <column>)" names both shapes even though only one applies to a given target.
- **Fix:** The board reads "The bot opens a card in Backlog for every item…". The channel reads "…a thread in #orders…". The rest of the sentence is verbatim.
- **Committed in:** 2de8e94f

**2. [Rule 3 - Blocking] Inline combobox instead of Popover + Command**
- **Found during:** Task 2
- **Issue:** The target picker uses `Select`, and the web has no `Command` component. A popover inside the editor sheet would also clip, and the phone keyboard would cover it.
- **Fix:** The option list renders inline under the field, following the 07.11 people picker and the AI-chat share picker, with People and Teams in `role="group"`. "Up to 5" is the disabled field's placeholder.
- **Committed in:** 5aad04c3

**3. [Rule 2 - Correctness] Moving a fan-out automation where fan-out isn't allowed**
- **Found during:** Task 1
- **Issue:** The API checks the resulting row. Changing a fan-out automation's target to a DM, or its trigger to a webhook, without turning fan-out off would get 400 `fan_out_not_allowed`.
- **Fix:** `fanOutForSave` sends `{ enabled: false }` with the stored caps in that case.
- **Committed in:** 2de8e94f

**4. [Rule 2 - a11y] The fan-out switch's accessible name**
- **Found during:** visual check
- **Issue:** The `role="switch"` element had no name of its own, because the label targets base-ui's hidden input.
- **Fix:** Added `aria-label` with the row's title.
- **Committed in:** 5aad04c3
