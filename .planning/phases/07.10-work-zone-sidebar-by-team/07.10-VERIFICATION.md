# Phase 07.10: Work Zone sidebar by team — Verification Report

**Phase Goal:** The Work Zone sidebar is organised by team, like the chat sidebar, and scales to many people and projects. It has:
- department sections, remembered per person, with the viewer's own departments first;
- only the team projects the viewer belongs to, each with a live count, opening its project page;
- at most 3 active sessions under each project, then "+N more";
- "Needs you" on top and "Personal" at the end;
- "+" per section and per project, with everything else reached through "Browse projects";
- the same component on desktop and in the mobile drawer, with low visual noise;
- plus closing the 07.9 D-14 project-name leak.
