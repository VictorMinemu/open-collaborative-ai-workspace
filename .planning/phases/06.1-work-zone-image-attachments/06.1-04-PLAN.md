## Phase Goal

A controller pastes, drops or attaches images in the Work Zone composer and the session's runtime (Claude Code or
Codex) receives them with the prompt; everyone in the session sees the sent images in the stream.

**This plan's slice:** the phase gate — the whole local regression on one fresh isolated stack, the documentation and
traceability, then the owner's check on real runtimes. Planner decisions: `06.1-RESEARCH.md` (P-01 … P-12); executor
rules P-13 in `06.1-01-PLAN.md`.
