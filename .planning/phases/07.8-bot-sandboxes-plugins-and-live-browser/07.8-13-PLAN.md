<threat_model>
## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| brw (member cookies) ↔ sbx (exec) | Separate containers on one bot network; no listening services |
| bot networks ↔ proxy ↔ outbound | The proxy is the only bridge |
| API ↔ broker frame/input streams | Internal, token-authenticated |

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-07.8-13-01 | Information disclosure | member cookies readable from the shell sandbox | high | mitigate | Separate `brw` container, no listening port, pipe-only CDP (Task 1 checks) |
| T-07.8-13-02 | Elevation of privilege | proxy on the control network | high | mitigate | Egress attached to bot and outbound networks only (Task 1) |
| T-07.8-13-03 | Information disclosure | input payloads or frames logged or buffered | high | mitigate | Pass-through, latest-frame-wins, spy logger test (Task 2) |
| T-07.8-13-04 | Denial of service | browsers held forever | medium | mitigate | 5-min idle, holds capped at 15 min, kill by label (Task 2) |
| T-07.8-13-05 | Information disclosure | cross-bot browser reach | high | mitigate | Per-bot networks; smoke connects A → B fail (Task 2) |
