# Phase 07.3 Plan 04: Modules panel, Settings › Modules and the phase gate Summary

Admins and unit leads now turn Work Zone, AI chats and Bots off and on from Settings. On a unit they do it for
everyone in it or for one person; admins also do it for the whole organization from the new Settings › Modules. Every
change applies live in the member's open tab. The account entry "AI chat" hides when AI chats is off. The phase gate is
green on one fresh isolated stack: 87/87 e2e, 2915/2915 verify tests, 274/274 integration tests and 320/320 node tests.

## What was built

- **`lib/module-access-queries.ts`.**
  - `useModuleAccess(scopeUnitId)` calls `GET /api/v1/module-access`.
  - `useSetModuleAccess(scopeUnitId)` calls `PUT`. The response replaces the scope's cache and the other scopes are
    invalidated.
  - Live: `permission_rules.updated`, `units.updated` and `member.updated` on `org:<id>` refetch every scope.
    `org-queries.ts` is untouched.
- **`module-access-card.tsx`.**
  - `ModuleAccessPanel` is pure. It renders one row per catalog module with the rail icon, label, description and a
    switch with a 44 px hit area, plus:
    - the "Custom" badge and its sentence;
    - for an inherited module, a read-only switch with "Off for everyone in X" / "Off for the whole organization",
      and a "Change" link only when the viewer can open that scope;
    - the amber Bots warning;
    - "Exceptions for people" with chips and Edit/View;
    - "Turned off inside X" with links.
  - `ModuleAccessCard` adds the query, the mutations, the pending spinner, toasts and three dialogs:
    - the confirmation;
    - "Add an exception" (searchable);
    - "Modules for <name>", with disabled switches and the reason when `canEdit` is false.
  - `UnitModulesCard` decides its own visibility from `unit.viewerCan`. `OrganizationModulesCard` serves Settings ›
    Modules and shows a 403 empty state.
  - `unit-detail.tsx` gets exactly one import and one mount line (+2/−0).
- **Settings.**
  - `Modules` sits after Organization (`Blocks`, `unit.modules.manage`, so admins only).
  - The account `AI chat` entry carries `ai_chat.use`, and `settings-sidebar.tsx` filters `ACCOUNT_SECTIONS` (see
    decision 1).
  - `/settings/ai-chat` is wrapped in `ModuleGate module="ai_chats"`.
  - The Settings › Devices subtitle reads "The computers you paired and who can use them" when `device.pair` is off
    (07.3-03 open item 1).
- **Docs.**
  - `docs/STATUS.md` holds the "Built so far" list, by phase, with 07.3.
  - DEMO.md has a "Module access" section: where, who, what off does, the admin sentence, Bots, what stays, and an
    owner check with the audit query.
  - AGENTS.md is down to 190 lines (see Task 3).
  - `07.3-VALIDATION.md` was created in the 07.1 format, filled and signed.

## Deviations from Plan

### Auto-fixed issues and choices

**1. [P-08 consistency] The account "AI chat" entry hides only on a decided `off`.**
- **Plan:** filter `ACCOUNT_SECTIONS` "exactly as it filters workspace sections" (`can(p)`).
- **Problem:** `can(p)` is false while permissions load and after a failed fetch, so every member would see "AI chat"
  flash in on each load, and lose it on a failed fetch. That goes against P-08 (only `off` hides).
- **What I did:** `ACCOUNT_SECTIONS.filter((s) => !s.permission || grainStatus(…) !== "off")`, the same rule 07.3-03
  used for `device.pair`. The acceptance grep still matches.

**2. [Rule 2 - correctness] An account entry and a role read for wording only.**
- `canEdit: false` covers an admin, but also a peer lead and the lead herself (the role ceiling is about equal ranks).
  "…for an admin" would be wrong for those.
- The card reads the member's role from `useMembers()` only to choose the sentence. The plan's sentence is used for
  admins; for others it is "Only an organization admin can change modules for <name>."
- Who may edit still comes only from `canEdit`.

**3. [Additive] Bots warning e2e and screenshots.**
- The plan asked for the warning but no e2e step covered it, because Core owns no bots.
- The lead test now creates a Core persona through the API and checks:
  - the row's warning ("Core … has 1 bot. …");
  - the confirmation title and sentence;
  - that Cancel writes no rows.
- The persona is deleted in `finally` and in teardown, because a unit persona blocks deleting the unit.
- This adds the `module-access-bots-*` screenshots (24 `module-access-*` files instead of 20).

**4. [Additive] Ada Stone, a second organization admin, is a member of Core.**

**5. [Rule 1 - spec] Mobile wait in my own spec.** After `goto("/chat")` the spec waited for `channel-list`, which on
phones only mounts in the drawer. It now waits for the channel URL. Fixed before cf68b03.

**6. [Scope] The Devices subtitle (07.3-03 open item 1) is in `devices/page.tsx` and `devices-settings.tsx`, outside
the plan's file list.**
- `DevicesSubtitle` follows `useGrain("device.pair")`.

- The orchestrator asked for this explicitly.

**7. [Doc] The plan's keyword check "must print nothing" on the current file. On the pre-edit AGENTS.md it printed
