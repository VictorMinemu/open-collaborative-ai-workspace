# Phase 03.2 Plan 05: Shared devices Summary

A device owner now shares a device from Settings › Devices with chosen people, teams or departments, each with a maximum mode. Listed people start sessions without the owner accepting each one. Removing someone or un-sharing stops the sessions that lost access, and lowering a cap downgrades live sessions. Each device card says whose Claude or ChatGPT login runs the sessions and shows the device's Full access opt-in as read-only.

## What was built

- **API (Tasks 1–2):** `device/grants.ts` adds list, put and delete routes. Principals are validated through RLS: an active member or an existing unit. Full access needs `devices.full_access_opt_in` (409 `device_opt_in`, message naming `ocw-runtime full-access on`). Removal is a soft revocation. Changes are audited as `device.grant_added`, `device.grant_updated` (with `previousMaxMode`) and `device.grant_removed`, and fire a `device.updated` event on `org:<id>`.
  - The device list fills `viewerMaxMode` from `loadDeviceAccess().capOf`. It fills `grants` only for the owner or an admin.
  - PATCH persists `shareMaxMode`, with the same opt-in gate. Its audit carries `previousShareMaxMode`.
  - Removals, lowered grants, `shared: false` and a lowered `shareMaxMode` call `onAccessChanged(…, "access_changed")` once the change is committed.
- **Web (Tasks 1, 3):** `DeviceSharing` replaces the old switch row in owner cards. It keeps the switch, now titled "Everyone in the organization", plus an always-visible max-mode select and the floor helper line. It also has the "Specific people and teams" list (avatar or unit icon, kind, mode select, remove with confirm), "Add people or teams" (search over members and units, excluding the owner and anyone already listed), and the note "People you share with use this subscription."
  - Every card has a subscription line per signed-in runtime ("runs on your / <owner>'s Claude login | ChatGPT login").
  - Every card has a read-only Full access row with the off/on copy and the ADR-0012 "dedicated machine" recommendation.
  - The Full access option is disabled with its reason until the device opts in.
- **Plan 04 open item:** `device.full_access` is non-delegable. Allow rules (org-wide or per user) never confer it, and rule validation refuses them. This was a real escalation path: the new resolve test was RED before the fix.

## Deviations from Plan

1. **[Rule 1 - Bug] A server-failed session was overwritten by the daemon's `completed`.** After `session.stop`, the real daemon reports `session.state: completed`, which turned the revoked session (and Plan 04's unauthorized-mode stops) from `failed` to `completed` and hid the reason. Found by the e2e. `applyDeviceState` now keeps `failed` when the device reports `completed`, with an integration assertion (`work-session/service.ts`, 5199ba1).
2. **`onAccessChanged` runs after the commit instead of "in the same transaction".** The handler opens its own tenant transaction and would not see uncommitted grant changes. This matches Plan 04's DELETE device → `onOffline` pattern. Per-turn checks cover the window in between: a revoked author is refused at dispatch.
3. **[Rule 2] Extras:** grants to the owner are refused with 400 `owner_principal`; the device row is locked (`FOR UPDATE`) on grant and PATCH changes; removal asks for confirmation; `loadGrants` is batched for the device list.
4. **Full access disabled in selects (Task 3 behaviour) shipped with Task 1's component.** The API refuses it either way.
5. **Plan 04 open item** (device.full_access non-delegable) done here, as instructed: commits b13b5fd and ae6053d.
6. **The Task 1 e2e went green only after Task 2's service fix.** The removal step of the artifact flow needed deviation 1.
