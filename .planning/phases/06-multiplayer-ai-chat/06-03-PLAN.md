## Phase Goal

**As a** member who holds a private AI chat, **I want to** share it in place with a colleague, a team, a department or the whole organization so that several of us and the model write in the same conversation — the model knowing who said what and answering one request at a time on the asker's own plan or model, nobody's private context leaking, and agents joining as full personas that post live cards — **so that** AI work in the workspace becomes multiplayer, attributable and safe.

**This plan's slice (tracer, browser level):** Acceptance Scenario B steps 1–4 in two browser contexts on the isolated harness — create a private chat → Share → "People and teams" → Alex, Can chat (the dialog lists what becomes visible) → Alex sees it under "Shared with me" → both write → the mock model replies "Hi Alex Kim! (participants: Alex Kim, Víctor Ruiz) …". 06-11 adds typing, presence, the queue and Ask AI; 06-12 completes the dialog (teams, departments, organization, levels, removal). P-decisions: `06-01-PLAN.md`.
