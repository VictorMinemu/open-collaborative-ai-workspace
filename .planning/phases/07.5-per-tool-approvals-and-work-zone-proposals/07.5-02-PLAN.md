## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-07.5-02-01 | Information disclosure | stored tool arguments across tenants | high | mitigate | Forced tenant RLS on the three tables, seeded cross-tenant test (Task 1) |
| T-07.5-02-02 | Tampering | a changed stored action being approved | high | mitigate | Hash recomputed from the stored row at every decision and grant use (07.2), so an edited row makes the request unusable; no update path is written in this phase |
| T-07.5-02-03 | Denial of service | oversized stored arguments or prompts | medium | mitigate | Byte and character checks in the database (Task 2) |
| T-07.5-02-04 | Tampering | duplicate requests from client retries | low | mitigate | Partial unique (event, tool_call_id) and the proposal tuple (Task 2) |
| T-07.5-02-05 | Elevation of privilege | a malformed rule letting a lead name arbitrary approvers at org scope | medium | mitigate | Approver-kind checks; scope validation is the API's (03) |
