## Phase Goal

**As an** Organization Admin and every member of the workspace, **I want to** arrange the company into departments, teams and projects with the four PRD roles, have every route decided by one permission resolver, and manage my own password, sessions and profile, **so that** each person and agent can do exactly what their role, units and rules allow and every sensitive change is attributed and audited.

**This plan's slice:** people administration — roles, deactivation, the member directory and profiles, and Team-Lead invitations (research R2–R4, R12, Pitfalls 1, 7).

<objective>
Extend `organization/members.ts` (created by 02-02) with the member directory including units and status, member profiles (ORG-06 "see other members' profiles"), role assignment with the last-admin invariant (ORG-02, D-06), deactivate/reactivate with session revocation; and move invitations onto the resolver with the role ceiling and the inviter relation (SC2: Team Leads invite and revoke). Invitations target the organization only (D-05).

Purpose: the API half of the admin console (ORG-04); 02-12 builds the pages on it.
Output: members.ts routes, invitations.ts on perms without the legacy preHandler, two integration test files.
</objective>
