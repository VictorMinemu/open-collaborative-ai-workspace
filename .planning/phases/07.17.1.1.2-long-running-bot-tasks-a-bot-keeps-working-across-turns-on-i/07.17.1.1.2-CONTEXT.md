# Phase 07.17.1.1.2: Long-running bot tasks - Context

**Gathered:** 2026-11-12
**Status:** Ready for planning (after 07.17.1.1.1)
**Mode:** owner request + `--auto` defaults. On ocw.autofy.ing (v0.3.1) the bot "Gertru", asked to read a 3,030-file
knowledge pack, answered: "No tengo una ejecución autónoma en segundo plano habilitada, así que no voy a decirte que
seguiré trabajando después de responder" and stopped after each turn; the person had to keep saying "continúa"/"haz la
tarea". Owner (verbatim): "ahora mismo dicen que no tienen habilitado la ejecución en segundo plano lo cual es muy malo;
el bot debe ser más proactivo y que haga las tareas y que pueda hacer tareas de larga duración." Ships in v0.3.2.

<domain>
## Phase Boundary

A bot can take on a task that needs many turns and keep working on it by itself until it is done, without a person
re-prompting: it continues its own work in the same conversation, it is woken when long sandbox processes or its Work
Zone sessions finish, it reports progress, and people can see and stop it. Bounded by budgets so it can never run away.

Not in this phase: new sandbox tools (07.17.1.1.1); new triggers beyond the bot's own continuation and process/session
wake-ups; cross-conversation tasks; multi-bot task delegation (ADR-0027 consults stay as they are).

</domain>

<decisions>
## Implementation Decisions

- **D-01: A task is a platform entity.** `bot_tasks` (org-scoped, RLS): bot, conversation/thread, the person it runs
  for (the asker who started it — they pay, ADR-0019's rule; their rights are re-decided every turn), goal (short text
  written by the bot), status (running / waiting / done / failed / stopped), progress note, turn count, started/updated
  times, budgets. Audit on start, each continuation, finish, stop.
- **D-02: The bot starts and continues with tools.** `ocw_task_start {goal}` (one active task per bot per thread) and
  `ocw_task_continue {progress, nextStep, afterSeconds?}`: the current turn ends and a new turn of the same bot is
  delivered in the same thread (worker job, like 07.16 `work_session.bot_sync` wake-ups) with the task's goal, progress
  and next step in its context. `ocw_task_finish {summary, outcome: done|blocked}` ends it; blocked mentions the person.
  Turns are delivered with the same tools and taint rules as a person's turn in that thread (taint persists across the
  task's turns once outside content was read).
- **D-03: Wake-ups.** A long sandbox process the bot started during a task (`ocw_sandbox_exec` background /
  `ocw_sandbox_process`) and its 07.16 Work Zone sessions wake the task when they finish (one wake per event, deduped),
  so the bot waits instead of polling. A task can also wait on a timer (`afterSeconds`, bounded).
- **D-04: Budgets (never runaway).** Per task: max turns (default 40, org-configurable up to 200), max wall time
  (default 8 h, up to 24 h), counted in the bot's daily turn limit; per organization: max running tasks (default 10).
  When a budget ends the task stops with a clear message in the thread mentioning the person. The org policy
  (Settings › Bot policy) has an "Allow long tasks" switch (default on) and the limits; per bot, the capabilities panel
  shows it like other capabilities.
- **D-05: Visible and stoppable.** A live task card in the thread (Agent Card, ADR-0020) shows goal, status, turns
  used/max, elapsed, last progress note, and a **Stop** button for the person it runs for, the thread's admins/leads and
  the bot's owner/admins. Progress messages are rate-limited (no spam): at most one visible progress update per N turns
  or minutes; the card updates in place.
- **D-06: Proactive by default.** The bot's instructions (the turn context / system guidance the plugin sends) tell it:
  when a request needs more work than one turn, start a task and keep going instead of asking "¿continúo?"; never claim
  background work it has not scheduled; report when done or blocked. This replaces today's "no background execution"
  behaviour.
- **D-07: Safety.** Same permission intersection every turn (the asker ∩ bot ∩ resources); a task never widens what a
  turn may do; after outside content the task keeps the taint (Work Zone starts follow ADR-0038's outside-content rule);
  stopping is immediate (in-flight turn finishes, no new turn is delivered); a disabled bot or a person who lost access
  stops the task. No new listening ports; ids-only jobs (pg-boss).

### Decided after research (2026-11-12, owner's autonomy grant; RESEARCH.md A1–A10 accepted)

- **D-08:** Tasks keep running after outside content; the taint persists on every task turn and the budgets stop them
  (Work Zone starts still follow ADR-0038's outside-content rule).
- **D-09:** Continuation turns have no browser tools but list the thread's attachments (07.15 rules).
- **D-10:** AI chats are out of scope in v1 (channels, threads and DMs only).
- **D-11:** At most one visible progress message every 5 turns or 10 minutes; the card updates in place every turn.
- **D-12:** A task turn that ends without `ocw_task_continue` or `ocw_task_finish` gets one automatic nudge turn; a
  second one in a row stops the task as blocked and mentions the person.
- **D-13:** Task tools are not side-effecting and do not count toward the 8 tool calls per turn.
- **D-14:** The plugin accepts a silent task turn (no text) when it called continue/finish, and reports `done` for task
  turns (`bridge.ts`); the persona prompt change (D-06) is shared with phase 07.17.1.1.1's toolkit paragraph — one edit
  site in `buildPersonaPrompt`, coordinated by plan order.

</decisions>
