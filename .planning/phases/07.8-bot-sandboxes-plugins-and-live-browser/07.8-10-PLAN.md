## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| sandbox files → conversation | `share` moves bytes out of the confined workspace |
| activity → readers | Commands and output tails become visible to every reader of the reply |

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-07.8-10-01 | Information disclosure | secrets in commands or output shown in activity | high | mitigate | 07.5 `maskForDisplay`, 20 lines / 4 KB tails, no env, hosts only (Task 1) |
| T-07.8-10-02 | Tampering | prompt-injected exfiltration through share | high | mitigate | share is side-effecting: refused after taint unless approved (Task 3) |
| T-07.8-10-03 | Information disclosure | activity to non-readers | medium | mitigate | Ephemeral event on the conversation topic (gateway authorization); attached to the reply only (Task 1) |
| T-07.8-10-04 | Repudiation | unaudited file changes | low | mitigate | Audit per write, delete and share with ids and sizes (Tasks 1–3) |
