# Phase 07.4 Plan 01: Per-member connector, keychain and template contracts Summary

**Every 07.4 shape now lives once in `@ocw/contracts`. That covers connector auth modes with OAuth URL derivation, write-only member and vault secrets, versioned agent templates, Agent Channel connector and http-request tools, and four new resolver grains. A recursive test checks that no response schema has a key that could carry a secret.**

## Performance

- **Duration:** about 52 min (17:02 to 17:54 local). Most of it was three full `pnpm verify` runs of about 12.5 min each.
- **Tasks:** 3 of 3.
- **Files:** 6 created, 13 modified.

## Accomplishments

- **Tracer (Task 1).** One vocabulary now covers the whole path of a per-member connector call:
  - `ConnectorAuthMode` on `McpServer` and on the create and update requests, with refinements:
    - stdio is refused, and the message names stdio;
    - organization headers are refused on per-member modes;
    - `per_member_secret` needs a header;
    - a printable-ASCII prefix is enforced, so CR and LF are refused;
    - an OAuth client and scopes are only allowed with `per_member_oauth`.
  - The three static `ocw_*` built-in tools.
  - The `member_account` withheld reason, with a `resource` for the share link (R7).
  - `AgentChannelConnectorCall`: a safe name `m` + 8 hex, arguments up to 32 KB, and any `userId` stripped.
  - Thirteen refusal codes.
- **Task 2.**
  - Member connector DTOs, including R1 `accountLabel` and R2 `reconnectReason`.
  - Shares and the readers preview.
  - The full keychain: handle, host patterns plus the `vaultHostAllowed()` matcher, injection with the forbidden-header helper, Basic injection forced for username-and-password keys, an https OAuth token URL that must be on an allowed host, and `valueUpdatedAt` (R3).
  - Templates: `InstantiateAgentTemplateResponse` reuses `Agent`.
  - The `ocw_http_request` body and response. The connector list also carries `keys`.
- **Task 3.**
  - `connector.connect` and `vault.use` (surface `bots`, held by every role).
  - `vault.manage` and `agent_template.manage` (surface `team`, held by admins and by leads on the subtree they lead).
  - Agents may hold only `vault.use`.
  - The Bots module now also covers `connector.connect` and `vault.use`.

## Broken-windows candidates

1. **Existing Bots-off rules no longer read as "off" (high).** `module-access.ts` (API) computes `off` only when every catalog pattern row exists. Organizations or units that turned Bots off before this change have 5 rows, so they now read as `custom`, and `connector.connect` and `vault.use` are not denied for them until someone re-toggles Bots. A backfill should add the two rows wherever all five former bots rows exist at the same scope and subject. The natural places are 07.4-02's migration (if it can still take it) or 07.4-14.
2. **`memberHeaderName` forbidden-header check (medium).** It must be enforced API-side in 07.4-06 and 07.4-10 with `isAllowedInjectionHeader()` (see Deviations).
3. **Test-environment flake (low).** `apps/api/src/modules/ai-chat/shares.int.test.ts` once hit a testcontainers Garage "ports to be bound" 10 s timeout; it passed on rerun.
4. **Pre-existing (out of scope).** 4 biome warnings in `apps/local-runtime` (`useOptionalChain`, `noUnusedPrivateClassMembers`).
