# Phase 07.10 Plan 04: Needs you, Settled, grouped search and keys Summary

The Work Zone sidebar now tells each person what waits on them, keeps history one click away and finds anything from
one field. On top, "Needs you" lists the approvals the viewer may decide (amber "Needs approval") and their own
finished turns they haven't opened yet ("Your turn", within 24 h). Rows leave live, with no toast, when the approval
is decided, the session is opened or a new turn starts. Settled is a quiet collapsed section limited to the viewer's
own sidebar scope. A query swaps the tree for grouped Projects, Sessions and Repositories. The sidebar works from the
keyboard, and focus stays in place when the focused row goes away.

## What the sidebar does now

- **Needs you (D-05, § 3):** `needsViewer(session, seen, now, openSessionId)` gives one of two reasons:
  - `approval` when the API's `attention.approval` is true, even while the session is open.
  - `turn` when the session is idle with an empty queue, the viewer's last turn ended within 24 h and after this
    browser last opened it, and it is not the open session.

  No `attention` means nothing. Only sessions in the model's scope count (PD-07), so an admin-only approval never shows.
  Approvals come first, then turns, most recent first; 5 rows, then "Show {n} more" / "Show fewer" (`needs-more`,
  `aria-expanded`). Each row shows a 16 px badge, the title, the 07.9 marker and the reason. The project (or repository)
  is named only in the sr-only `row-project` and the hover `title`. The link's name is
  "{title}, {reason}, in {place}". Rows are 44 px below 768. A collapsed department keeps the project and the sessions
  that need the viewer.
- **Seen map (R10, PD-09):** stored in `ocw.workzone.seen.v1` as `{ id: isoTime }` (ids and times only). It is pruned to
  7 days and 300 entries on every write. Corrupt JSON reads as empty, a failing storage falls back to memory, other
  tabs follow through the `storage` event, and nothing is sent. `useLiveSession` marks a session seen when its detail
  loads, and when a frame ends a turn (a prompt completes or fails, or the session turns idle), always at the later of
  now and the server's time. `session-view.tsx` is unchanged.
- **Settled (D-04, § 8):** a section at the end showing the scope's settled sessions, most recent first.
  - Collapsed by default, remembered with the other sections, with no count pill.
  - Rows are the existing `SettledRow`: 10, then "Show {n} more" / "Show fewer".
  - It keeps the `settled-group`, `settled-toggle` and `settled-more` ids.
- **Search (D-08, § 11, C-01):** text is case- and accent-folded, and exact prefixes come first. The groups are:
  - Projects: every index project. Managed ones are muted, and no row has a "+" (X5). 5, then "Show all {n}".
  - Sessions: every listed session, matched on its title, repository or a project the viewer can see named. Active
    sessions use the needs-you look with their status; settled ones use `SettledRow`. 10, then "Show all {n}".
  - Repositories: personal repositories and hidden-project repositories, with the hint and no "+". 5, then
    "Show all {n}".

  A polite `role="status"` says "{n} results" 300 ms after typing stops. With no match the sidebar reads
  "Nothing matches “{q}”.". The header, the search field and the foot stay in place.
- **States:** the first load shows one skeleton (a header bar and three rows). When the sessions request fails, the
  sidebar shows "Couldn't load sessions." with Try again (`sessions-error`, `sessions-retry`) and keeps the project
  rows, without counts or sub-rows. "No sessions yet." appears only when there is no project, no repository and no
  session.
- **Touch (R9):** the search field is `max-md:h-11 max-md:text-base` (16 px, no iOS zoom), the header "+" is
  `max-md:size-11`, and the clear-search button has a 44 px target below 768.
- **Keys (§ Keyboard):** a handler on the nav, with no roving tabindex.
  - ↑/↓ move between section toggles, links and `aria-expanded` buttons; "+" buttons and repository rows are skipped.
  - Home and End jump to the first and last row. ArrowUp on the first row returns to the search field.
  - ←/→ on a toggle collapse and expand its section.
  - ArrowDown in the search field enters the results or the rows. Escape clears the query and keeps focus in the field.
  - A MutationObserver keeps focus nearby: when the focused row leaves, focus moves to the next row of its section,
    else to the section's toggle, else to the same place in the sidebar.

## Open risks (for 06)

- "Your turn" is per browser (U-4): on another browser or device, a turn already seen elsewhere shows once until it is
  opened there. A server read cursor stays deferred.
- Needs you draws from the model's scope, which needs the projects index. While `/work-projects` loads, a team project's
  sessions that need the viewer appear only once it arrives (the viewer's own and personal ones appear at once).
- `searchSidebar` scans every listed session on each keystroke (memoised per query). This is fine for hundreds of
  sessions; not measured at thousands.
- Focus survival relies on the row element being removed. A row whose reason changes in place (React reuses the
  element) keeps focus, which is the intended outcome.
