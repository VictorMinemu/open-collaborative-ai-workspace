and executor rules in 07.2-01-PLAN.md).

the bot's write is refused because the turn is tainted, it proposes into Sam's private memory, and Sam approves the
platform-owned card in the DM with a note; then every card state and the screenshots. Split out of 07.2-13 at revision

anything in this plan. Reconciled here: R4 (decision note in the card dialogs), R6 (visible preview, status labels from
`approval.status`, requester line).
